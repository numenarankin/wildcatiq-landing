## 6. Artificial Intelligence Features

The Services include artificial intelligence ("AI") features, including our assistant, Orion. This section explains what information those features use, which AI providers receive it, and what controls are available. The AI providers we use are also listed in Section 9.

### Orion Assistant

Orion answers questions, drafts content, and helps you work with your organization's information by chat or by voice. To do this, Orion reads Customer Data through your signed-in session, within your organization's data and the AI settings your Administrator has chosen. Depending on those settings, Orion can work with information such as wells, production, facilities, leases and owners, people and vendors, inventory, accounting and prices, tasks and calendar, files, your past Orion conversations, and team messages.

When you use Orion, we send your message, the conversation history, relevant Customer Data, and your personalization information (described below) to Anthropic, which provides the AI models that power Orion. Files you attach to an Orion message are sent to Anthropic for that exchange. We keep the file name and type in the conversation, but not the file itself.

**Web search.** Orion can search the web to answer a question. When it does, the search queries it writes, which may reflect the content of your conversation, are processed through Anthropic's web search service.

**Team messages.** Orion can search and read only the direct messages and group conversations that you are a member of. This is on by default, and an Administrator can turn it off. Orion cannot send team messages or rename team conversations unless an Administrator turns that ability on.

**Support conversations.** Orion cannot read, send, or update your support conversations with us unless an Administrator turns that ability on.

**Your conversations.** Your Orion conversations are not visible to other users in your organization. You can delete a conversation at any time. Deleting a conversation does not remove memory or personalization that Orion has already learned from it (see "Memory and Personalization" below).

### Voice Features

When you speak to Orion, your recorded audio is sent to ElevenLabs to be converted to text, and when Orion speaks a reply, the reply text is sent to ElevenLabs to produce the audio. We do not store your audio recordings. The text of a voice conversation is saved as an Orion conversation, just like a typed chat.

In the mobile app, you can also dictate into text fields, such as notes and comments. Your audio is sent through our servers to ElevenLabs in the same way, and the resulting text is placed in the field for you to review.

### Memory and Personalization

Orion learns from your conversations so it can serve you better. After each chat or voice exchange, Orion automatically reviews the exchange (using Anthropic) and may update the following without asking you first:

- **Your memory**, which holds facts about you and your operation;
- **Your persona**, which describes how you prefer Orion to respond; and
- **Your playbooks**, which record procedures you repeat.

Your memory, persona, and playbooks are tied to you within your organization, and other users in your organization cannot see them. You can view, edit, and restore earlier versions of your memory and persona in your AI settings. We keep every prior version, so editing or clearing your memory or persona does not remove earlier versions. Playbooks cannot currently be viewed or deleted in the Services.

Some AI memory is shared within your organization:

- **Remembered facts.** When you approve Orion's request to remember a specific fact, that fact is stored for your organization and may be used when Orion helps other users in your organization who have permission to use AI.
- **Project memory.** Orion keeps an evolving summary of each project, updated after conversations in that project. It is available to users in your organization who have permission to use AI and can see that project.

To store and find remembered facts, we send them to Mistral AI, which converts the text into a numeric form used for search. Remembered facts cannot currently be viewed or deleted in the Services. To request deletion of your memory, persona, playbooks, or remembered facts, contact us as described in Section 15. [PROCESS FOR DELETING AI MEMORY ON REQUEST]

### Document Scanning and Search Indexing

**Search indexing.** When a PDF or image is uploaded to the Services, including a file synced from a connected Dropbox account, we send it to Mistral AI to read its text (optical character recognition). We save the extracted text with the file. We then send that text, and the text of uploaded plain-text files such as CSV and TXT files, to Mistral AI to convert it into a numeric form that lets Orion and search features find relevant documents. Search queries are converted the same way. Other file types, such as Word and Excel files, and very large files are stored without being indexed. Indexing happens for every eligible upload, even when document scanning is turned off.

**Document scanning.** When document scanning is on, uploaded documents are sent to Anthropic to read and to identify records they contain, such as readings, tickets, invoices, and settlement statements. Scanning applies to uploads by any user who can upload files, whether or not that user has permission to use AI. Document scanning is on by default, and an Administrator can turn it off. When it is off, uploads are not scanned for records, but they are still indexed for search as described above.

**Human approval.** Document scanning never adds or changes records on its own. It produces proposed entries that a user must review and approve before anything is saved. Likewise, Orion adds or changes records, sends messages, or remembers facts only after you approve each action. Orion cannot delete records.

AI does update some information automatically without approval: your memory, persona, and playbooks, project memory, the extracted text saved with uploaded files, and images Orion creates (see "Images" below).

### Images

Orion can create images from a text description using Google's Gemini models. Only the text description is sent to Google. Generated images are saved automatically as files in your organization's account.

When a user chooses to digitize a scanned well log image, the image is sent to Google's Gemini models to read it.

### AI Summaries

We use Anthropic to write summaries from Customer Data, such as dashboard briefings and the summaries included in scheduled reports, digests, and some exported reports. These summaries may be emailed to the recipients your organization chooses (see Sections 8 and 9).

### Administrator Controls

A Customer's Administrators control how AI is used in their organization:

- Users must have permission to use AI in order to chat with Orion, use voice features or dictation, or see dashboard briefings. Administrators grant and remove this permission.
- Administrators can turn off specific types of Orion access, such as reading or sending team messages or working with particular kinds of data.
- Administrators can turn document scanning on or off.

Some Orion capabilities cannot be turned off, including web search, public records lookups (see Section 7), image creation, Orion's memory, and general analysis. Search indexing of uploads also cannot be turned off.

### Optional Providers

## 7. Public Records and Third-Party Sources

Some information in the Services does not come from you or the Customer. It comes from public government records, from records we obtain from public agencies, or from third-party services a Customer chooses to connect. This section explains those sources.

### Texas Railroad Commission Records

The Services include research and lookup features built on public records published by the Railroad Commission of Texas. We copy these records from the Commission's public data downloads and public map services into a separate database hosted by one of our service providers (see Section 9), and we refresh that copy regularly. The records cover operators, wells, leases, fields, drilling permits, completions, injection and disposal, purchaser and gatherer connections, plugging, and well tests.

Some of these records include personal information that the Commission publishes, such as:

- **Operator records:** operator names, mailing addresses, phone numbers, registration dates, and registration status.
- **Officer records:** names, titles, and addresses of officers listed on an operator's filings with the Commission, along with the operators they are associated with.

We use these records to:

- Let signed-in users search and browse operators, leases, wells, and the people listed as operator officers, including by a person's name.
- Power map layers, well records, lease history, and the data used to prepare regulatory report files.
- Help a new Customer find its own operator record during setup and bring its wells and leases into its account.
- Answer questions in our AI assistant, Orion, which can search and read these records, including by a person's name. Results Orion retrieves become part of the conversation and are processed as described in Section 6.

During setup, to help identify a new Customer's operator record, we use the company name, your full name, your phone number, and the ZIP code from the company address you entered as search terms against these public records. If you enter your operator number, we look up that number first.

We obtain mineral ownership rolls from county appraisal districts. We currently hold the roll for Archer County, Texas, which we obtained from the Archer County Appraisal District.

These rolls include personal information such as owner names (including co-owners), owner type (for example, individual, company, trust, or estate), mailing addresses, the royalty, working, or overriding interests an owner holds in each lease, acreage, and appraised values. We use this data to let signed-in users search for mineral owners, view an owner's holdings and mailing address, see the owners of interests in a lease, and ask Orion about mineral owners.
