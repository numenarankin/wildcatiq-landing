# Ramp - landing page reference

## Structure
- Slogan hero ("Time is money. Save both.") with two CTAs (free start + demo), a funding announcement banner, and a "switch in days" secondary hook.
- About 12 sections, nearly all AI/agent framed: platform overview, an interactive "paste your URL for AI recommendations" form, a pain contrast ("Systems that never spoke"), three agent-product reveals (Stack, Intelligence, Policy Agents) each paired with one testimonial, demo CTA, enterprise and global blocks, a testimonial wall, closing CTA that repeats the hero slogan.
- Value-prop first with features as links (Cards & Expenses, Procure to pay, Accounting automation, Banking, 200+ integrations); outcome claims (3.2x growth) over feature depth.
- Social proof is the heaviest of the set: 85,000+ teams, logo wall, about 20 attributed quotes spanning startups, enterprise, schools, and a city government.
- No pricing on the home page; links to Pricing. Very large footer with free-tools column.
