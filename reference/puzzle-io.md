# Puzzle - landing page reference

## Structure
- Announcement-style hero ("Introducing Puzzle 2.0") with dual CTA (demo + free trial) and offer badges (14-day trial, 10,000+ companies).
- About 12 sections, framed as a two-track product (software vs software + services) and a four-way "pick your path" plan chooser.
- Feature framing is product-suite first (AI Chat, AI Cowork, MCP), then services tiers, then platform/integrations/guarantee as trust blocks.
- Social proof is stats + a carousel of 10 customer-story headlines rather than quotes.
- Pricing appears on the home page (from $30/mo software, from $99/mo services) plus a money-back guarantee.

## Title and meta
- Title: Puzzle: AI Accounting Software & Accounting Services
- Meta description: Run your books with Puzzle's AI suite or add a Puzzle bookkeeper for expert review or a fully managed close. Real-time financials on one platform.

## Hero
- Headline: Introducing Puzzle 2.0: AI-native accounting software + Puzzle accounting services
- Subhead: You can now get both: Puzzle's AI-native accounting software, plus Puzzle accounting services layered on whenever you need them.
- CTAs: Book a demo, See Puzzle in action, Chat with an expert, Try for free, Sign in
- Badges: 14-day free trial; add accounting services; 10,000+ companies

## Section: Two ways to run your books
- Software: You close the books; AI handles categorization, reconciliation, and drafting. Includes AI Chat, Cowork, and Close, plus real-time cash, burn, and runway. CTA: Try for free.
- Software + Services (New): Puzzle accountants or a vetted partner firm add a monthly review or a fully managed close. CTA: Explore services.

## Section: The Puzzle AI suite
- Heading: Ask anything. Puzzle prepares the work. You approve it.
- AI Chat: Answers questions about your books in plain English.
- AI Cowork: Agents prepare tasks (matching invoices, finding subscriptions, chasing receipts). Nothing changes until you approve.
- Puzzle MCP: Connects Claude or ChatGPT to your books.
- CTAs: Learn about Puzzle MCP, Try the AI suite for free

## Section: Your books. Your way. With an expert when you need one. (Puzzle Services)
- Expert Reviewed: A bookkeeper reviews and corrects AI output and closes books monthly. Eligible businesses under $10K monthly expenses.
- Full Service: A named bookkeeper handles the monthly close, including accruals, prepaids, and statements.
- Project services: Migration, catch-up bookkeeping, R&D study, tax filing.
- CTAs: Explore services, Chat with an expert

## Section: A simple place to start (choosing a plan)
Four paths: do it yourself (software + AI suite); someone to check your work (Expert Reviewed); someone to handle the books (Full Service); a specific project. CTA: Compare plans and pricing.

## Section: The platform underneath
Real-time general ledger with cash and accrual views.

## Section: Integrations
Direct syncs with Stripe, Mercury, Brex, Ramp, Gusto, Rippling, Deel, Bill.com, plus 12,000+ banks via Plaid.
