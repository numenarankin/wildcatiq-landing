## Section: FAQ (10)
- What is the Puzzle AI Suite? AI tools in Puzzle's platform (Chat, Cowork, Close) that work on the live general ledger.
- What can I ask AI Chat? Any business finance question in plain English, answered from actual books.
- What does AI Cowork do? Prepares routine tasks: unused subscriptions, invoice matching, receipt tracking, vendor flags, document prep. Nothing final until approved.
- What is AI Close? AI-assisted month-end close for accountants managing books on Puzzle, with human review and sign-off at each step.
- Can Puzzle's AI change my books without my approval? No. Chat is read-only, Cowork output needs approval, AI Close keeps accountant review.
- How accurate is Puzzle's AI? Same ledger, rules engine, reconciliations as the books; answers trace to inspectable transactions.
- Is the AI Suite included in Puzzle's plans? Yes, every software plan from Starter to Scale; free trial. (/pricing)
- What's the difference between the AI Suite and Puzzle MCP? AI Suite works inside Puzzle; MCP connects Puzzle data to external assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT. (/api-mcp)
- Can I use the AI Suite and still have an accountant manage my books? Yes; self-service, expert help, or full handoff. (/pricing?tab=accounting)
- Is my financial data secure? SOC 2 compliant, encryption, access controls; data does not move to another tool for AI. (/security)
