## Section: The Problem - The Cash-to-Accrual Gap Creates Manual Work
- Founders: cash shows whether they can hire, spend, or grow; investors, auditors, and lenders need accrual-basis books.
- Firms: legacy systems push accrual work (prepaids, depreciation, deferred revenue, amortization) into spreadsheets; closes happen outside the ledger.
- Legacy systems force separate cash and accrual workflows, with offline adjustments and reconciliation at every close.

## Section: Automated Lifecycle - Continuous Ledger Lifecycle
- 1 Transaction: a bank feed triggers an expense or revenue event
- 2 Schedule: a schedule is drafted in real time
- 3 Monthly Entries: generated unsummarized
- 4 Review: the firm approves schedules before posting
- 5 Post: the ledger posts monthly and updates both views

## Section: How Puzzle Eliminates Manual Accrual Work (comparison table)
- Prepaid Expenses: offline spreadsheets and summarized entries -> automated full-lifecycle schedules with traceable monthly entries
- Revenue Recognition: spreadsheet waterfalls -> continuous contract-level schedules linked to contracts
- Fixed Assets: offline calculations -> auto-detected schedules with per-asset depreciation entries
- Automated Bill Matching: manual matching of bank feeds to bills -> automatic matching of incoming payments
- Accrual Adjustments: delete, recalculate, repost -> automatic recalculation of downstream entries
- Reporting Views: separate trial balances -> one ledger with a single toggle between cash and accrual

## Section: How Puzzle Works: Built Into the Ledger, Not Bolted On
- Legacy platforms calculate accruals outside the ledger; Puzzle drafts schedules and journal entries inside it.

## Section: Questions we hear a lot. (FAQ)
- How does Puzzle handle accrual schedules? Many amortization and depreciation types; once approved, both views update continuously; view, edit, or cancel.
- Can firms customize amortization and depreciation policies? Yes: default methods, useful-life guidelines, capitalization thresholds.
- What happens when a transaction changes after schedules are created? Adjust schedules in the UI; downstream impacts update without manually deleting journal entries.
- Does Puzzle maintain both cash and accrual reporting simultaneously? Yes, from the same transaction data.
- How are accrual entries reviewed before posting? New schedules need initial approval; subsequent entries post automatically until the schedule ends or is modified (e.g. asset disposal).
