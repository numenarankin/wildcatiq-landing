## Section: Problem statement
- Your client just flagged an error. In a board meeting. A duplicate or miscategorization went unnoticed until the client found it; Puzzle runs natively inside the system instead.

## Section: Six layers of continuous confidence
- Intro: automated checks that keep the ledger audit-ready
- 1 Vendor Review: flags inconsistent categorization of known vendors and suggests a mapping to approve
- 2 Transaction Review: consolidated feed for transactions needing human review, with filtering and bulk approval
- 3 Flux Analysis: compares performance across custom date ranges and segments, highlighting variances above user-set thresholds
- 4 Month-End Close Review: guided checklist run by customizable AI agents, with high/medium/low confidence tags and pre-drafted next steps
- 5 Pre-Drafting Reconciliations: agents match bank feeds to ledger entries and pre-draft reconciliations for sign-off
- 6 Ledger Health Scans: continuous scanning for double-postings, unusual amounts, suspicious descriptions

## Section: Accuracy Built Into Every Step (How It Works)
- Continuous Scanning: agents review transactions, bank feeds, and vendor histories in the background
- Smart Flagging: edge cases surface in the dashboard with an explanation of why they were flagged
- Review and Approve: review mappings, approve reconciliations, resolve flags; the agent prepares the work
