## Section: FAQ (12)

- Who does the accounting work? Puzzle accountants or a vetted partner firm, in Puzzle, on the same live data, every change visible.
- Expert Reviewed vs. Full Service? Expert Reviewed is AI bookkeeping with monthly expert review; Full Service is a named bookkeeper running the entire close.
- What's included in Expert Reviewed? Monthly close, transaction review, reconciliation checks, up to three questions per month, for businesses with up to $10K monthly expenses.

- How much do services cost? Expert Reviewed $99/month per entity; Full Service from $499/month per entity; both require a Puzzle software plan.
- Do I need a software subscription? Yes; services can't be bought alone.
- Which service is right? Just raised: Full Service; second opinion: Expert Reviewed; months behind: catch-up; switching from QuickBooks: migration; tax deadline: tax filing.
- Can Puzzle catch up behind books? Yes.
- Can I move from QuickBooks or Xero? Yes, through migration.
- Does Puzzle file taxes and R&D credits? Yes.
- Can I start with software and add services later? Yes, without rebuilding anything.
