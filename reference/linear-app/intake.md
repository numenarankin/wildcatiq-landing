## Section: Linear Agent. Issue intake, fully automated
- Lead: Automatically turn Slack conversations, customer support interactions, and sales calls into actionable issues that instantly get routed to the right team. Link: Linear Agent+
- Mock: ENG-2704 Track iOS startup performance, 2 / 15
- Create issues at the speed of conversation: Mention @Linear in discussions on Slack or Microsoft Teams, and the Linear agent will create issues informed by your conversation's context. Mock commands: "@Linear create new issue and delegate to Cursor"; "@Linear assign to alex and delegate to Copilot"; "@Linear add to UI refresh project and give it to Codex"
- From intent to PR with a single command: Turn Slack and Microsoft Teams threads into issues and assign them directly to a coding agent. No handoffs required.
- Turn support tickets into issues with a single click: Auto-create well-scoped issues from Intercom and Zendesk conversations – even across long, multi-topic threads.
- Auto-create issues from customer calls: Automatically capture product feedback from Gong transcripts and route them into Linear. No prompts or clicks required.
- Create requests and bug reports from anywhere: Integrate Linear with the rest of your tools to create issues directly from your CRM, monitoring system, email, and more.
