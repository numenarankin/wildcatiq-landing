## Section: Bring the voice of the customer into product development
- Tabs: Support tools, CRM tools
- Make customer feedback accessible: Capture feedback across any customer interaction – from sales calls to support chats – and turn it into a customer request linked to a Linear project or issue. Customer Requests is seamlessly integrated with the tools you already use, so you can create requests directly from your support platform, email client, CRM tool, or shared Slack channels.
- Mock: Intercom · zoe@acme.inc "We need a cost breakdown across all cloud platforms in a single report or dashboard" -> ACME New Request: Multi-cloud cost dashboard, Planning, Q4 2025, 17
- Quote: "Customer Requests closes the gap between our product and go-to-market teams. Our Sales and Success orgs now track feature requests at a customer level, and feedback flows directly into our development workflow." (no attribution shown)

## Section: Connect product instincts with customer insights
- Lead: Customer Requests aligns product work with user needs. Refine your product intuition with detailed customer feedback and realtime customer data to make better decisions.
- Mock: Customers 13 · Important 2 · Total revenue $47.8K · ACME, XMP, Echo, Unreal
- Prioritize your roadmap based on real demand: Segment and filter projects by request volume, customer tier, or revenue to identify and prioritize the most impactful feature work.
- Enrich requests with realtime customer data: Sync customer attributes such as company size, tier, or revenue from Salesforce and other systems of record.
- Understand customer needs in full contextual detail: Requests are directly attached to projects and issues, giving product teams full visibility into what customers are really asking for.
- Notification mock: Idle resource detection · New request from XMP | Multi-cloud cost dashboard · Requested project completed | ENG-943 Budget alerts · 25th customer requesting issue | IOS-396 Alerts on mobile · Requested issue completed
- Stay in the loop with every customer: Subscribe to individual customers and get realtime notifications on their requests – new and completed.
- Auto-prioritize requests from high-impact customers: Create triage rules based on custom fields in Salesforce to route and prioritize incoming requests.

## Section: Customer Requests works seamlessly with the rest of the Linear system
- Linear Asks: Create customer requests from emails and shared Slack channels.
- Linear Developers: Build your own add-ons and integrations with the Linear API.
- Product Intelligence: Use AI to auto-apply the right team, labels, and projects to new requests.
