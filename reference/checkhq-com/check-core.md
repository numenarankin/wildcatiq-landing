## Section: Money movement / Fully managed payments
- Body: With nationwide Money Transmitter Licensure and infrastructure built for reliability, Check ensures workers and tax agencies get paid on time, every time.
- Predictable payroll
  - Fast & flexible payment speeds: Help customers manage their cash-flow with confidence by offering 3-day, 2-day, or next-day processing.
  - Real-time visibility: Eliminate guesswork with live, API-powered updates that keep companies and employees informed at every step.
- Robust payment tools: Keep customers' payroll on track with reliable features to resolve common issues and prevent payment disruptions.
  - Easily unwind mistakes: Reverse employee payments and recover employer failed funding attempts to ensure workers are paid accurately and on time.
  - Self-serve voids: Reduce the friction of canceling approved payrolls with built-in refund handling and compliance guardrails.
  - Corrections made simple: Fix errors quickly with simple workflows that handle payment adjustments and tax amendments automatically.
- Zero risk processing
  - 100% loss coverage: Full coverage from credit and fraud losses on every payroll transaction so you can operate with peace of mind.
  - Intelligent monitoring: End-to-end monitoring across systems, identities, and payments detects risks early and protects your customers.

## Section: Tax Engine / Clear and compliant tax management
- Body: Check intelligently calculates and files more than 6,000 taxes across 1,000+ jurisdictions nationwide.
- Confidence in every filing
  - Readiness at a glance: View real-time filing statuses and blockers to resolve issues before taxes are due.
  - Draft return previews: Verify return details and make any necessary corrections before filings are submitted.
- Compliance on autopilot
  - Live regulatory monitoring: Tax law changes are captured automatically via continuous monitoring of federal, state, and local agency requirements.
  - Smart compliance guardrails: Prevent invalid filings with built-in checks for schedules, rates, and wage bases across jurisdictions.
- Intelligent automation: Smarter tax operations infrastructure helps teams detect and correct issues before they escalate.
  - Custom variance detection: Keep balances accurate with automatic adjustments for tax anomalies before they impact filings.
  - AI tax notice triage: Accelerate resolutions with AI parsers that read incoming notices, classify issues, and route tickets to the proper queue.
  - Automated refiles: Streamline operations with automatic generation of original and amended returns as your customers' data is updated.

  - Chain complex questions: Investigate a question across records for answers that actually connect the dots.
- AI that takes real action
  - Change anything with a prompt: Add employees, run payrolls, unblock tax filings, retry payments, and more.
  - Resolve issues end-to-end: From corrections to reports, AI completes the task instead of handing it back to you half-finished.

  - Create intelligent customer experiences: Build agentic payroll support experiences directly into your product by connecting your support agent to Checkmate and the Check MCP.
