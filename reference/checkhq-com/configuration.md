## Section: Flexible Rules / Tailor to any team
- Body: Customizable logic lets you support diverse business models, unique worker profiles, and up to 2,500 employees per company.
- Solo vendors to national ventures

  - Broad industry support: Equip your platform with built-in support for specialized tax codes, like nonprofits (501(c)(3)) and agriculture (H-2A).

## Section: Support any workforce
- Body: Fully configurable payment and tax rules let you adapt to however your customers pay their employees.
- Unlimited earning structures: Configure piece-rate, tipped, commission, or seasonal earnings with built-in tax calculation compliance.
- Flexible pay schedules: Customize payroll cadences and run off-cycle payrolls for adjustments, commissions, or bonuses.
- Custom benefits and deduction: Support company-specific programs and policies with flexible benefits and deductions modeling.
- Multi-state and multi-location ready
  - Advanced location management: Assign multiple workplaces per employee, split wages by location, and consolidate reporting with ease.
  - Automated reciprocity handling: Cross-state withholding scenarios and other special tax cases are fully managed on your behalf.
