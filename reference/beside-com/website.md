## Section: Everything you need to publish
- Lead: Built for you, then yours to change: domain, look, edits, and a booking path that hits the real calendar.
- Custom Domain: Your name on the URL, not a Beside subdomain
- Themes: Pick a look and swap it without a rebuild
- Design Mode: Click the page and move things yourself
- Prompt to make edits: Tell Beside what to change. It drafts the edit
- Booking Link: Customers book from the site, not a third-party widget
- Calendar Sync: Open times from the calendar you already run
- Tap-to-call: The number above the fold, on a line that answers
- Hours, name, and phone: The same details as the door and the Google listing
- Service pages: What you actually do, in the words customers type
- Work examples: Photos that stay true as the shop changes
- Kept current: Hours, crews, and pages update without a webmaster
- SEO-ready pages: Written so Google and AI answers can cite you

## Section: Your questions, covered (FAQ)
- Do I have to move off my current site? Not on day one. Run the free website audit, fix the listing, and keep the site you have until you are ready. The waitlist includes site build and maintenance for businesses that want Beside to own it.
- Will it help me rank on Google? A clear NAP, fast pages, and service pages that match how people search are the basics. Beside also pairs the site with reviews, ads, and the phone line that converts the click.
- Can I try something today? Yes. The free website audit flags the first fixes: tap-to-call, NAP match, and review markup. No signup to run it.
- Is website builder on the phone plan? The shipped phone product is line plus receptionist. Full site build and maintenance is part of Beside, on the waitlist. The audit tool is free for anyone.
- What about blogs and AI search? SEO and GEO pages are on the roadmap. The job is the same: be the business that wins the click in Google and in the answers customers now ask ChatGPT.
