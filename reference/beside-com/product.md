# Beside - All products hub reference

## Structure
- One-screen index: eyebrow, H1, two-sentence framing, then a 7-card grid where each card is the product name plus the exact first two sentences of that product page's hero. No images, CTAs, or FAQ. Each card links to the feature page.
- Useful as the canonical one-liner per product; the header mega-menu uses a shorter 5-to-8-word version of each.

## Title and meta

- Meta description: Explore the Beside agent workspace and standalone Beside Phone. Find product details for your website, follow-ups, business calls, and messages.

## Hero
- Eyebrow: Product
- Headline: What Beside runs.
- Subhead: Beside is the agent workspace for work across your business. Beside Phone is the standalone app for your business calls and messages. Explore each capability below for details on how it works.

## Section: product cards
- Business Phone (-> /phone): A real number for calls and texts, with the AI receptionist on the same line. Port or forward. Callers keep dialing what they already know.
- Website Builder (-> /product/website): Beside writes the site from the business you already are, then keeps hours, phone, and work examples current. No webmaster.
- SEO and GEO (-> /product/seo): Local search still starts on Google. More of it now ends inside ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Beside keeps your listing, pages, and answers consistent so you get the call either way.
- Ads Manager (-> /product/ads-manager): Campaigns set up, watched, and trimmed against real booked work, not vanity clicks. The number in the ad is a Beside line, so every lead is answered.
- Reviews Manager (-> /product/reviews): Unanswered reviews are the first thing a new customer reads. Beside drafts a reply in your voice, ready to paste, and keeps the listing from going quiet.
- Scheduling (-> /product/scheduling): The receptionist checks open slots, offers two times, and texts the confirmation. You stay on the job. The calendar stays true.
- CRM (-> /product/crm): Name, number, job, and transcript land as a contact without anyone typing. Follow-up stays on that thread until it is booked or dead.
