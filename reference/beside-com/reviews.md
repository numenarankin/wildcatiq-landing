## Section: Your questions, covered (FAQ)
- Does Beside post reviews for me? The free tool never posts. You copy and paste. Beside can draft replies and wait for approval before anything goes to Google.
- What about bad reviews? Acknowledge the issue, take it offline, and do not argue in public. The drafts follow the same rules Beside uses in production.
- Do replies help rankings? Owner replies are a local ranking and conversion signal. They also show the next customer you are paying attention.
- Is there a monthly review product fee? The reply writer is free. Review handling inside Beside is part of that waitlist product. The phone plan is separate.
- Can I use this if I am not on Beside yet? Yes. Type the business name, get drafts, paste. Call the live demo if you also want the missed calls handled.
