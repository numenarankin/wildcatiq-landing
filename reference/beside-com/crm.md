## Section: Your questions, covered (FAQ)
- Do I have to leave my CRM? No. Beside logs calls, summaries, and contacts into the tools you already use. You can also run a simple list inside Beside if you do not have a CRM yet.
- Which CRMs connect? HubSpot and a set of dispatch and inbox tools. See the integrations directory for the live list. More land as the system ships.
- Is this a full CRM like Salesforce? It is the layer that writes itself from the phone. If you already live in a heavy CRM, Beside fills it. If you do not, it is enough to stop losing names.
- What about follow-up texts? Beside drafts follow-ups for quiet leads. You approve the first ones. Repeat the same move and it offers to take it over.
- Does the $29 plan include CRM logging? Call summaries, transcripts, and contacts on the line are included. Deeper lead follow-up across email and campaigns is on the waitlist.
