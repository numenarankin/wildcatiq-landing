## Section: Your questions, covered (FAQ)
- Do I have to spend a minimum with Google? Google sets its own ad spend. Beside does not mark up clicks. Management is part of the waitlist for Beside; the phone line that answers the ad is available today.
- What is the Beside Ad Credit? One business from the first 1,000 waitlist signups receives $1,000 in Beside credits toward the growth plan its audit lays out. Official rules sit under the waitlist form.
- Can Beside change my campaigns without asking? Routine optimizations can run once you trust them. Anything with your name or budget attached starts as a draft you approve.
- Will this replace my current agency? If an agency already books you profitable jobs, keep them. Beside is for owners who cannot staff a media buyer and still need the phone answered when the ad hits.
- How is this different from boosting a Facebook post? Google captures people already looking for the job. Beside then answers the call. Boosted posts are a different intent and a different page.
