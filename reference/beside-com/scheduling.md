## Section: Your questions, covered (FAQ)
- Which calendars work? Google Calendar is the common path. You can also set bookable hours inside Beside without a full calendar sync.
- Can it book different jobs at different lengths? Yes. A leak and a water-heater install are not the same slot. You define services and duration. The receptionist offers what matches the call.
- What if I need to approve first? You can collect the request and confirm yourself, or let it book inside the rules you wrote. Same trust model as the rest of Beside.
- Does this replace my dispatcher? It takes the intake and the first slot. Dispatch still owns routing, parts, and who rolls. The summary is waiting when they look.
- Is scheduling extra? Booking from the call is part of the AI receptionist on the phone plans. Deeper calendar autonomy is part of the waitlist for Beside.
