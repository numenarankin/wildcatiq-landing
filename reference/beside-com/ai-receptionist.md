## Section: Your questions, covered (FAQ)
- What is an AI receptionist? An AI receptionist answers your business line the moment a call comes in, talks to the caller like a person on your team, captures the job details, and books the appointment. Beside does this 24/7, including nights, weekends, and the hours you are already on a job.
- How is Beside different from voicemail? Voicemail asks the caller to wait. Most of them hang up. Beside's AI receptionist has a live conversation, qualifies the work, and puts a booking on your calendar. You get a summary and a transcript, not a pile of missed-call callbacks.
- Does the AI receptionist book appointments? Yes. It checks your calendar, offers open times, and confirms the booking with the caller. You can set the services it can book, the hours it should offer, and when a call should transfer to a person on your team.
- Do I have to change my phone number? No. Port your existing number to Beside or forward calls from your current carrier. Callers keep dialing the same number.
- What does the AI receptionist cost? Beside's phone product is $29 per business per month for 30 AI-answered calls, or $99 per business per month for unlimited AI-answered calls. Every other feature is included on both plans. No per-minute fees.
- Can I try it before I sign up? Yes. Call the live demo line and talk to a real Beside AI receptionist. Ask it anything, act like a customer, try to break it. No meeting required.
- How is this different from an AI answering service? They are the same Beside product described two ways. AI receptionist is the person who answers, qualifies, and books. AI answering service is the coverage: after hours, overflow, and 24/7. If you searched for answering service, this is the page.
