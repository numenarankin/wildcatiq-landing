## Section: Your questions, covered (FAQ)
- Where do I log in to my existing Phone account? Choose Open Beside Phone on this page. Use the same email address, phone number, or sign-in method you already use. You can also install the iOS or Android app from the download page and log in there.
- Is Beside Phone still available? Yes. Beside Phone is available for existing customers and for businesses that want a standalone business phone with an AI Receptionist. Phone plans and features are at beside.com/phone/pricing.
- How does Beside Phone relate to Beside? Beside is the agent workspace for work across your business. Beside Phone is the app for your business calls and messages. Use the shared login to see the products and businesses available to your account.
- Where can I get help with my number, app, or billing? Visit the help center for Phone setup and support. Manage a web subscription in your Phone account. If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, manage that subscription in the same store.
