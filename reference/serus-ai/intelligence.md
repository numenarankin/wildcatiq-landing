## Section: Frequently asked (accordion)
- What can I investigate with Serus? People, accounts, domains, wallets, phone numbers, and suspicious activity. Start with whatever you have - an email, a username, a screenshot, or a question in plain language - and Serus determines the right investigation path.
- What sources does an OSINT search cover? Sources including the surface web, public records, social profiles, breach databases, paste sites, and specialist OSINT sources, in a single pass. Every finding is attributed to the source it came from, so you can verify the whole picture.
- Does Serus search the dark web? Yes. Dark web scans include breach dumps, credential collections, forums, and market listings. Sensitive values like passwords stay redacted until you explicitly choose to reveal them.
- What are deployed agents? Your own monitored-source feed: autonomous agents that watch names, domains, and keywords around the clock and report the moment something new surfaces - no re-running searches by hand.
- Can I build Serus into my own systems? Yes. The developer API lets you verify a key, start scans, pull results, and stream findings as they surface, documented against OpenAPI 3.1. An MCP toolkit for AI agents is coming soon.
- Is what I search private? Yes. Nothing you search is used to train AI models, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never sell your data. You can export or delete everything at any time.
