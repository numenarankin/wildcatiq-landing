## Section: Frequently asked
- What is an OSINT investigation? Research built from open sources: websites, public records, social profiles, breach data and the like. The work is connecting scattered identifiers (an email, a handle, a domain) into one picture you can verify.
- What are OSINT tools? Tools that search and connect open-source information. Many do one job each, like a username search or a breach check. Serus runs the searches together and cross-references the results into one verified profile, instead of scattered pages you connect by hand.
- Does Serus search the dark web? Yes. Dark web scans include breach dumps, credential collections, forums and market listings. Sensitive values like passwords stay redacted until you choose to reveal them.
- Can I build Serus into my own systems? Yes. The developer API lets you start scans, pull results and stream findings as they surface, documented against OpenAPI 3.1.
- Is using OSINT legal? Working with publicly available information is legal in most places, but how you use it is governed by law, including privacy rules like the GDPR and anti-stalking and anti-harassment laws. You're responsible for having a lawful basis for your searches.
