## Section: Frequently asked (accordion)
- What does a Serus scan find? One scan sweeps data brokers, breach dumps, public records, social profiles, and the dark web, then collects everything tied to your identity - addresses, phone numbers, leaked credentials, aliases - into a single exposure list, ranked by risk.
- How does Autopilot removal work? The moment an exposure is found, Serus files the removal request with the source's data controller - GDPR Article 17 and CCPA where they apply - then follows up until it comes down. You can watch every request move through the pipeline in the app.
- How long do removals take? Most data brokers respond within days rather than the weeks a manual opt-out takes. Some sites are slower or re-list data later, which is why surveillance keeps running after the first removal and re-files automatically.
- What happens if my data comes back? Round-the-clock surveillance watches every source Serus monitors. The instant your data resurfaces - a relist, a new breach, a fresh listing - you're alerted and a new removal is already being filed.
- Do I need to hand over a lot of personal information? You start with the basics - name and email - and add details like phone numbers or addresses only if you want those searched too. More coverage finds more exposure, but you decide what to include, and nothing you provide trains AI or gets sold.
- Can I protect my family or my team? Yes. You can onboard multiple people, map each person's exposure, and manage removals from one dashboard - the same way security teams protect executives and other high-risk staff.
