# What is Snitcher?

> Snitcher is a B2B visitor identification platform that reveals which companies visit your website using IP intelligence—no form submission required.

**Snitcher is a B2B visitor identification platform that reveals which companies visit your website—even before they fill out a form.** Using IP intelligence and first-party tracking, Snitcher identifies anonymous companies, tracks user behavior across sessions, and syncs lead data to your CRM in real-time.

## How Snitcher Works

We use **first-party website data** to identify companies across your entire digital presence: marketing site, blog, documentation, and application. Your sales and marketing teams can then respond to buying intent in real-time.

When a visitor lands on your website:

1. **Company Identification**: If the visitor is browsing from a business network, Snitcher identifies their company using IP intelligence—no form submission required.

2. **User Identification**: If the visitor has ever provided an email (via form, signup, or login), we associate their session with that identity for precise, person-level attribution.

3. **Session Tracking**: Every pageview, event, and conversion is captured and tied to the identified company or user.

4. **Real-Time Alerts**: Your team gets notified via Slack, email, or CRM when high-value prospects show buying intent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

> **How does Snitcher identify companies without a form submission?**
>
> Snitcher uses IP intelligence to match visitor IP addresses against a proprietary database of known business networks. When someone visits from a company's office or corporate VPN, we identify the company instantly—no cookies or forms required.

> **Is Snitcher GDPR compliant?**
>
> Yes. Snitcher processes company-level data (not personal data) for IP identification, which is based on legitimate interest. For user identification, you control when to call the identify method and should obtain proper consent. Snitcher integrates with all major cookie consent platforms. See our [Cookie Consent guide](https://docs.snitcher.com/product/tracker/cookie-consent.md).

> **How is Snitcher different from Google Analytics?**
>
> Google Analytics shows you anonymous traffic metrics—pageviews, sessions, bounce rates. Snitcher tells you *which companies* are behind that traffic. You can see that "Acme Corp visited your pricing page 5 times this week" rather than just "5 visits from California."

> **Which CRMs does Snitcher integrate with?**
>
> Snitcher integrates with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM, Microsoft Dynamics, Attio, and Close. We also support Slack, Microsoft Teams, and automation platforms like Zapier and webhooks for custom integrations.
