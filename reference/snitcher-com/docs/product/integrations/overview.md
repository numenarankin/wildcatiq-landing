# Integrations Overview

> Snitcher integrates with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Slack, Zapier, and 15+ other tools. Sync identified companies to your CRM and automate workflows.

**Snitcher integrates with CRMs, communication tools, and automation platforms to help you act on identified website visitors.** Push companies and contacts to HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive in real-time. Get Slack alerts when target accounts visit. Build custom workflows with Zapier or webhooks.
