## Section: FAQ (14)
- What is accounts payable software? Automates invoice-to-payment: capture, matching, approvals, payment, ERP sync.
- What is Ramp Bill Pay? AI-powered AP with OCR, PO matching, approval workflows, multiple payment methods, ERP sync (NetSuite, QuickBooks, Sage Intacct, others).
- Can Ramp Bill Pay be used standalone? Yes, without Ramp cards or expense features; syncs with 10 ERPs.
- How do I set up Ramp Bill Pay? Connect accounting provider (optional), import or request vendors, link a bank account, choose payment methods, set approval flows and permissions.
- What are Ramp AP agents and how do they work? Autonomous agents for end-to-end AP: auto-coding, fraud prevention, approval, automatic payment.
- What payment options are available? Standard ACH, Same-Day ACH, Ramp card, check, domestic/international wire; card payments via existing card or one-time virtual card.
- How does it handle vendor setup and payment information? Vendor ACH details extracted from invoices or requested; W-9s uploaded or requested; tax data exported as CSV; vendors don't need a Ramp account.
- Does Ramp support recurring bill payments and amortization? Yes; NetSuite amortization templates synced and applied.
- Can I generate an AP aging report? Yes, CSV download of unpaid bills, amounts owed, overdue amounts.
- Can I import purchase orders? Yes, from NetSuite or QuickBooks; 2-way and 3-way matching.
- What features should you look for in AP software? OCR capture, PO matching, custom approvals, real-time ERP sync, multiple payment methods, vendor portal, audit trails, reporting, ease of use, scalability.
- Why Ramp / who it's best for: small businesses, mid-sized companies, multi-entity organizations.
- Pricing: free tier, no monthly software fee. Per-transaction: standard ACH $0.59; standard check $1.99; same-day ACH $10; domestic wire $15; SWIFT USD wire $20; overnight check $20; check attachments $1 per page. Ramp Plus $15 per user per month plus platform fee; annual billing saves 20%; Enterprise pricing available. Eligible fees waived when paying from a Ramp Checking Account (except overnight checks and attachments).
