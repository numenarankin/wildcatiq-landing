## Section: FAQ (5)
- What is a corporate card and how does it work? Company-issued charge card; the company, not the employee, is liable. Ramp adds spend controls, approvals, real-time tracking.
- Do corporate cards affect your personal credit score? Usually not; Ramp cards carry no personal guarantee. (-> /blog/business-credit-cards-no-personal-guarantee)
- Who is eligible for a corporate credit card? Registered businesses; Ramp requires no personal guarantee or annual fee and issues employee cards once approved.
- What's the difference between a corporate card and a business credit card? Corporate cards suit mid-size to large companies, underwritten against the business; business cards often require a personal guarantee. (-> /business-credit-cards)
- What makes Ramp one of the best corporate credit cards? Built-in expense management, automatic categorization and receipt matching, real-time policy enforcement, cashback, no annual fees or interest.
