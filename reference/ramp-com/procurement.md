- Your entire process, automated: parallel approval routing, rules by budget, department, or vendor type, automatic vendor onboarding.
- AI Agents handle compliance checks: contract reviews, security assessments, and other evaluations before the request reaches an inbox.
- Your team stays in their tools: Jira for security approvals, Ironclad for legal review. (See all integrations -> /integrations)

## Section: Contract Renewals - Renew on your terms.
- Nothing renews without sign-off: tracks agreements, reminders before deadlines, full approval for renewal decisions.
- Get a negotiation briefing instantly: pricing benchmarks, flags agreements worth renegotiating, recommends extend, renegotiate, or cancel.

## Section: Purchase Orders - Every dollar matched. Every error caught.
- Don't get overbilled a cent: automatic 2- and 3-way matching, discrepancy flags, overbilling alerts.
- Capture card payments automatically: matches card transactions and can issue cards directly from a PO.
