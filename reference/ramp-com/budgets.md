## Section: FAQ (7)
- What is Ramp Budgets? Compares plan to actuals in real time across cards, reimbursements, Bill Pay, committed POs; nests up to five levels; maps to ERP fields; feeds approval flows.
- Who can access it? Plus and Enterprise plans.
- How do I get started? Build a structure, upload a budget, map line items under Manage Spend > Budgets; guide and video available.
- What spend does it map? All Ramp spend types in real time, including committed PO spend.
- What fields can budgets map to? Any Ramp field incl. ERP fields (department, entity, GL account, project) and custom roll-up fields.
- Can it block spend at the limit? No. Tracks plan vs. actuals only; card controls, approval workflows, or Funds handle limits; budget-aware approvals flag spend that would push a line over budget.
- Who should use it? Teams wanting real-time visibility, budget context in approvals, delegated control, especially multi-entity.
