## Section: FAQ (6)
- How does Ramp's expense management solution help businesses save time and money? Cards plus expense management, real-time visibility, mobile submission, receipt matching, coding, policy enforced before spend.
- Can it integrate with my accounting system? Yes: NetSuite, QuickBooks, Xero, Sage, others; categorized expenses sync in real time.
- How does Ramp ensure expense policy compliance? Limits and restrictions by merchant, category, amount; receipt and memo requirements; approval workflows; violations flagged or blocked; Policy Agent reviews every expense and escalates judgment calls. (-> /blog/ramp-agents-announcement)
- What automation features simplify expense approvals? Rules by amount, department, vendor; Policy Agent can approve in-policy expenses. (-> /intelligence)
- Does Ramp support employee reimbursements? Yes; submit via web, mobile, SMS, Slack, Teams; paid to bank accounts in 1 to 2 business days.
- How does Ramp help track, control, and categorize employee expenses? Receipts captured or matched, memos prefilled, coded at purchase, synced to ERP; dashboards show trends and outliers.
