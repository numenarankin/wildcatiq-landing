## Section: Feature carousel (4)
- Book travel without second-guessing. Policy status shown for every option.
- Know what you can spend before you spend it. Per diem adjusts by location and trip length.
- Leave your computer in the hotel room. Policy checks and trip questions via SMS, Slack, or the Ramp app.
- No receipt searching, saving, or uploads required. Receipts matched, transactions coded, memos suggested.

## Section: Never overpay for your stay.
- Ramp rebooks automatically when a rate drops by $50 or more.

## Section: Custom travel policies enforced at booking.
- Policies vary by location, duration, and department (business class for long flights, economy for local trips).

## Section: Route approval requests to the right person, at the right time.
- Avoid approval bottlenecks while staying compliant.

## Section: Every trip and travel dollar in one place.

## Section: Always know where your team is.
- Monitors active and upcoming trips for emergency outreach.

## Section: Book with any travel provider, Ramp automates the rest.
