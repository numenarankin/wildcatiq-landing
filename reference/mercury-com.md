# Mercury - landing page reference

## Structure
- Brand-attitude hero ("Radically different banking") with email capture plus Open account / Launch demo CTAs and a product announcement banner (Mercury Payroll).
- About 9 sections: four value pillars, testimonials, stats + press band, four themed feature groups (each a headline with 3 to 4 sub-features), a trust/security block, a closing CTA.
- Framing is benefit-headline first ("Banking's been a headache. Now, it's a head start."), with features as proof under each benefit; the "all in one place" pillar grid is the only straight product list.
- Social proof is heavy: four founder quotes with name/title/company/vertical, four stats, three press callouts; no customer logo wall.
- Pricing on the home page: $0/month business, 3.50% APY personal, link to full Pricing page. Fintech disclaimer in footer.
