# Rho - landing page reference

## Structure
- Positioning hero ("Business banking for the AI era") with three CTAs (Open Account, Request Demo, AI Demo) and a human-support promise in the subhead.
- About 8 sections: scale promise, customer testimonials, 8-tile product grid, a $0 fee table, implementation speed, bank-partner safety block, founder testimonials, closing CTA.
- Framing alternates product-grid and trust: features are listed plainly (Incorporation through Close), while two separate testimonial sections lean on service and relationship rather than software.
- Social proof is quotes-heavy (4 attributed customer quotes with logos, 6 founder anecdotes) plus yield/cashback/insurance numbers.
- Pricing is on the home page as a fee table (all $0 except 1% FX); no subscription or per-seat pricing.
