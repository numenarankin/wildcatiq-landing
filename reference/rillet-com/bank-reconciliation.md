## Section: Built to handle the messy reality of accounting
- Power of AI: Our proprietary machine learning models will automatically match 95%+ of the incoming bank transactions with your invoices and bills. It eliminates the need to match thousands of transactions manually every month, allowing you to close your books faster.
- Rillet x Stripe: Tech companies of all sizes across B2B and B2C rely heavily on Stripe to collect payments. With Rillet, all your payments, returns, voids and fees information are directly synced from Stripe and reconciled.
- Automated Quick Entries: Tired of reconciling thousands of small transactions from recurring vendors every month? Rillet automates this process by posting journal entries with pre-defined general ledger accounts and reporting categories. In case of a new vendor, Rillet AI automatically suggests the relevant coding for the transaction.
- Live Bank Transactions Feed: Rillet connects you with 12,000+ financial institutions through Plaid, ensuring a live feed for all your bank transactions. Rillet has custom integrations with banks not connected through Plaid such as JP Morgan Access, HSBC and more.
