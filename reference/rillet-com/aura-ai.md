# Rillet - Aura AI product page reference

## Structure
- The one "advanced feature" page that breaks Rillet's product-page template: it adds a logo strip under the hero, a three-pillar trust block, a four-part feature walkthrough (Assistant / Agents / Continuous AI / MCP, same four tabs the homepage shows), one customer quote, and an architecture diagram before the shared "Explore more" grid and closing CTA.
- About 8 sections. Each of the four Aura surfaces gets a label, an H3, two or three sentences, and one visual; the three pillars are one sentence each. No FAQ, no stats, no tabs or accordions (static blocks with "See more" links).
- Copy to screenshot ratio is roughly even: 1 hero visual, 4 feature visuals, 1 architecture diagram, 1 customer photo, 12 logos shown twice. Copy carries the argument; images illustrate.
- Positioning is "not a chatbot": emphasis on live GL data, accountant-trained agents, reviewability and audit trail.
- Hands off to adjacent features via a 6-card "Explore more Rillet features" grid (title + one line + thumbnail), then the site-wide "zero-day close" closing CTA.

## Section: Aura, the AI that learns your books.
- Intro: Aura runs on your live general ledger and remembers how you work, so it answers, acts, and reconciles the way your team would.
- Assistant / Ask your GL anything: Natural language queries against your live books. Ask Aura about vendors, accounts, balances, or any cut of your data. Get back numbers you can trust, sourced from the GL. (visual alt: Ask anything. Instant answers.)
- Agents / Complete accounting tasks end-to-end: Reliable, repeatable workflow agents built to accomplish sophisticated tasks. Write the rules in plain English. Aura handles the schedule, the data pulls, and the handoffs. (visual alt: Structure, visibility, repeatability)
- Continuous AI / Always on, always working: Context-aware intelligence assisting your close. Aura watches for anomalies, flags exceptions, and proposes accruals as they happen. You see issues before they become close-week fires.
- MCP / Your current numbers, anywhere you work.: Connect Aura to Salesforce, Snowflake, Claude, or any BI tool. Analyze your live books from the tools you already use, with one secure surface for reads and writes.
