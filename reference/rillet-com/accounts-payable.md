## Section: Benefit tiles
- Enjoy automated bank reconciliation: Bills get automatically reconciled against their bank transaction by Rillet's AI, saving hours per month of manual processing per month.
- Track your 1099s: Record your contractors information and when January comes, just export the 1099 report with all information needed to complete your filings.
- Track VAT/GST tax information: Track your sales taxes directly in Rillet with the built-in reports by tax type and location, making tax season a seamless experience. Or connect Rillet directly with your tax tools such as Avalara or Anrok.

## Section: Built to handle the messy reality of accounting
- Automated prepaid expenses schedule: Paying for a service spanning over multiple months? No need to track your prepaid expenses schedule in excel any longer. Rillet leverages the service period of the bills to automatically post the journal entries to the relevant prepaid expense account.
- Integration with your AP tool of choice: Chart of account and custom reporting categories are pushed to your AP tool for direct tagging at the source. Bills and credit card transactions are synchronized in Rillet and posted to the general ledger without additional human intervention.
- Automated Quick Entries: Having hundreds of credit card payments to the same vendors? Enable the "Quick Entries" and let Rillet automatically create journal entries for bank transactions.
- AI-generated accruals: Rillet AI analyzes past vendors bills and service period to predict accruals to post for the next month. Just ask for the list of accruals, review it, and have Rillet AI do all the posting. No manual effort, just fast results.
