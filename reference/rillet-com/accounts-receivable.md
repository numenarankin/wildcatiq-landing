## Section: Built to handle the messy reality of accounting
- Automated invoicing: Rillet automatically generates an invoice schedule based on the details of the contract, which can be further customized to fit the customer needs. With automated reminders and online payment links on invoices, you can make it easier for your customers to pay you.
- Rillet x Stripe: Already using Stripe to invoice your customers? No problem. Rillet natively integrates with Stripe and synchronizes all contracts, invoices, products and customers. Payments are automatically reconciled with the corresponding invoices and journal entries are booked to the general ledger without human intervention.
- Usage-based invoices: Create your usage-based contracts and upload the customer usage per period through the API or csv. Rillet automatically generates the invoices, sends them to customers, and charges their payment method if they have one registered for automatic payment.
- Create standalone invoices with ease: Not all customer invoices are attached to a contract. Standalone invoices can be created manually, loaded through a template file or pushed via Rillet's public API.
- AR aging report on the fly: Regardless of the source of invoices or the loading mechanism, Rillet becomes your single source of truth for your accounts receivable. With a clear AR aging report, tracking late payers is done at glance.
