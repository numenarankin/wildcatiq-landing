## Section: FAQ
- What is Mercury Invoicing? Send invoices, track payments, and get paid, all within Mercury banking.
- How do I get Mercury Invoicing? Invoicing tab under Workflows for customers; non-customers can apply.
- Which payment methods can I enable? Credit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay (via Stripe); ACH or wire instructions; ACH debit (subscribers only, customer opt-in); Pay with Mercury.
- What does it cost? Creating and tracking invoices is free. Recurring invoices and branding require paid plans. Stripe charges about 2.9% + $0.30 per card payment. International transaction fee on non-USD card transactions.
- Can I record an invoice paid elsewhere? Yes, mark it paid with date and method.
- Does it integrate with accounting software? Bank feed imports to QuickBooks, Xero, and NetSuite, plus CSV downloads.
- Who can request and manage invoices? All admins by default, plus custom permissions.
