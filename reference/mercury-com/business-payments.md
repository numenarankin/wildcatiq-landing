## Section: FAQ
- Are ACH and domestic wires free? Yes, to send and receive.
- Are real-time payments free? Yes on Mercury; the other provider may charge fees.
- Can you send non-USD international wires? Yes, to 40+ currencies. 1% conversion fee on the USD amount. Processed within 1 to 3 business days; delivery can take up to 5.
- Can you pay 1099 contractors? Yes, by ACH, wire, or check. W-9 collection and 1099 filing free for Plus/Pro subscribers, or $5 per form.
- Do vendors need a Mercury account? No. Enter their details or send a secure invite.
- How do approvals work? Approval policies by dollar amount, non-admins can approve, separation of duties enforced, approvals cover payments and reimbursements.
- Can you move an existing bill pay setup? Yes: add recipients manually, invite them, import via CSV, or forward bills to a dedicated Mercury email address.
- Does Mercury offer checks? Yes. Subscribers can order up to six free checkbooks per year or use a third-party provider; a check review feature helps prevent fraud. Mailed checks via the Send Money flow.
