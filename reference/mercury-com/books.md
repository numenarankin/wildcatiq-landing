## Section: FAQ
- What is Mercury Books? Full double-entry accounting built into your Mercury account, capturing banking, cards, invoicing, and bill pay with no imports or manual entry.
- Does it replace QuickBooks? Yes. A complete system meant to replace other tools, not sync with them.
- Do I need a Mercury account? Yes. Turn it on from your dashboard once approved.
- Does it work with external accounts? Connects to thousands of external banks and cards, plus Stripe, PayPal, Gusto, with more coming.
- How much does it cost? Free for all of 2026, then $35/month.
- Can my accountant have access? Yes, role-based permissions, or be matched with a vetted partner. No cap on advisor seats.
