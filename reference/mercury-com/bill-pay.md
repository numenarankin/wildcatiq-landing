## Section: Feature run
- Handle all your bills with precision: built-in software reduces manual bill work
- Stay on top of bill management: bills sent to a designated email land in a bill inbox, sorted by approval status and due date
- Harness AI to process bills faster: bill details auto-populate on arrival so you can cross-check them
- Never pay the same bill twice: duplicate bills detected automatically
- Control your funds from end to end: bills paid from checking or savings, no third-party processor (FDIC footnote)
- Hold on to your money longer: payments go out when needed, supporting cash flow
- Pay the way you want: ACH, domestic and international wire, or check
- Approve bills effortlessly: multi-layered approval rules; approve in Slack or the mobile app
- Prevent unauthorized payments: only known vendors can send bills
- Close the books in record time: real-time data and automatic syncing with NetSuite, QuickBooks, and Xero
- Save time on manual data entry: bills categorized at payment sync to accounting software
- Exit out of your endless tabs: all bills and transactions viewable in one place

## Section: FAQ
- What is Mercury Bill Pay? An automated accounts payable solution for paying bills from your Mercury account with no middleman.
- How do I get Mercury Bill Pay? Available to Mercury customers via the Bill Pay tab (business) or Bill Pay under Payments (personal).
- What does it cost? Free for unlimited bills through business and personal accounts.
- Bill Pay vs. a standard payment? Bill Pay adds AI data extraction, a bill inbox, and accounting sync.
- Payment options? ACH, domestic and international wire, or check.
- Accounting integrations? GL-code sync to QuickBooks and Xero. NetSuite automations and CSV exports available; paid plans from $35/month.
- W-9 storage? W-9s and W-8 BENs requested during onboarding and stored in the dashboard.
- 1099 preparation? W-9s parsed into a downloadable CSV. Only W-9s can be parsed; parsing is third-party; Mercury does not provide tax advice.
