## Section: FAQ
- What do I need to apply? Formation documents, EIN document (or Form SS-4 if pending), government ID for operating controllers and 25%+ owners. US-formed company with US operations and a permitted principal address; some countries excluded.
- Where are funds kept and how is FDIC coverage provided? Choice Financial Group or Column N.A. via sweep networks, up to $5M across up to 20 banks.
- Can I apply from outside the US? Yes, for US-formed companies, subject to the same restrictions.
- What does it cost? No minimum balance, overdraft, monthly, or account-opening fees. Some features carry fees. Paid plans start at $35/month.
- Are ACH and domestic wires free? Yes, to send and receive.
- Are real-time payments free? Yes on Mercury; the other provider may charge.
- Does Mercury offer checks? Up to six complimentary checkbooks per year (or third-party), plus mailed checks via Send Money.
- Is Mercury a real bank? No. A fintech company that received OCC conditional approval in April 2026 to establish Mercury Bank, N.A. Banking is provided by partner banks.
