## Section: If you have questions, we have answers (FAQ)
- How do I sign up for IO? Open a Mercury account first. No separate application, no credit check. Lower balances start with daily repayment; $15K unlocks 30-day terms.
- How do I earn 1.5% cashback? Spend anywhere Mastercard credit is accepted. Cashback is deposited after you pay your balance.
- What will my credit limit be? Based on Mercury balances and optionally linked external accounts. Limits can rise or fall; reassessed after large withdrawals or deposits.
- How often do I need to pay off my IO balance? Charge card: full balance due each period, daily for new customers or monthly once balances reach $15K. Early payments allowed.
- Does IO have any fees? No annual fees and no interest. International transactions incur a non-refundable fee. Paid Mercury plans start at $35/month, mainly for NetSuite automations.
- What expense management features does IO have? Physical and virtual cards, card-only access, spend limits, merchant locks, receipt policies, receipt matching, subscription monitoring.
- How is IO related to Mercury? IO is part of Mercury's suite: banking, Treasury, debit and credit cards, reimbursements, spend controls, payments, venture debt, Investor Database. Mercury is a fintech company, not an FDIC-insured bank.
- Does IO build business credit? Mercury reports IO payment history to Experian, Equifax, and Dun & Bradstreet.
- What's the difference between the IO Card and a traditional credit card? IO is a charge card requiring full repayment; no interest, no revolving debt.
