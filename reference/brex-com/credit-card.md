## Section: FAQ (17)
- What is a corporate card? Cards issued to employees against a company limit, based on business financials, with card-level controls. Brex issues unlimited virtual cards instantly.
- How is it different from a business credit card? Business cards are underwritten on the owner's personal credit; corporate cards are issued to the company and built for controls and larger teams.
- Is a personal guarantee required? No; applications use an EIN and rely on business financials.
- Does it affect personal credit? No personal bureau check or reporting.
- Who qualifies? U.S.-incorporated companies, including startups, LLCs, and eligible nonprofits.
- How long for approval? Minutes for approved applicants; virtual card usable immediately; physical card mailed within days.
- How are limits set? Revenue, amount raised, or cash balance rather than FICO; up to 30x higher than traditional business cards.
- What spend controls exist? Policy enforcement at swipe by category, merchant, amount; automated receipts; dynamic approvals; anomaly flags.
- What security features? Fraud monitoring and compliance; SOC 1 Type I, SOC 2 Type II, PCI-DSS compliant.
- What rewards are offered? Over $350,000 in partner perks; 7x rideshare, 4x Brex prepaid travel, 3x restaurants, 2x software, 1x everything else.
- What software integrates? Native ERP and accounting integrations, plus built-in global travel program.
- Are rewards taxable? Spend-based rewards generally treated as purchase rebates; bonuses not tied to spend may be taxable. "This isn't tax advice; consult your tax professional."
- Is it accepted internationally? Yes, Mastercard, 210+ countries and territories.
- Foreign transaction fees? None, but FX markup up to 3% may apply; local-currency cards and billing in 60 countries avoid this.
- Which currencies? Local-currency cards in 60 countries and 30+ currencies.
- Best for startups? Up to 30x higher limits, local collections in 48 countries, no personal guarantee, unlimited cards, rewards, 24/7 fraud monitoring, Apple Pay.
- Good for an LLC? Yes: EIN-only applications, no personal guarantee; venture-backed LLCs can qualify with relatively low cash reserves.
