## Section: FAQ (15)
- What is spend management software? Centralizes and optimizes all company spending beyond basic reimbursements; Brex lets you pre-approve spend with embedded limits.
- What is the difference between spend management and expense management? Expense management tracks after spending; spend management is broader and proactive.
- What does spend management software do? Automates purchase requests, approvals, payments, receipts, budgets, reporting; Brex adds AI and real-time ERP sync.
- Does it integrate with accounting software? Yes: NetSuite, QuickBooks, Xero, Sage Intacct, real-time sync.
- Is it secure? SOC 1 Type I, SOC 2 Type II, PCI-DSS Level 1; aligned with FINRA, ITGC, NYDFS.
- Can it issue virtual cards? Unlimited virtual cards with specific limits and automatic receipt matching.
- How does it help with budgets? Real-time tracking, alerts near limits, automatic caps; budgets by team, project, or merchant category.
- How does it enforce spending policies? Rules embedded in payment workflows block violations at purchase; exceptions routed to approvers.
- Can it set spending limits? By employee, team, project, merchant category, or time period.
- How does it save money? Policy enforcement, duplicate payment detection, vendor negotiation data, fewer errors.
- Does it support bill pay? Emailed invoices processed with data extraction, approvals, and ACH, wire, check, or virtual card payments.
- How long to implement? HRIS-based provisioning, AI-assisted GL mapping, pre-built ERP integrations.
- Can it scale? Startups to enterprises across multiple countries.
- What types of companies use it? All sizes and industries.
- Does it provide real-time visibility? Dashboards show spend against budgets, pending approvals, policy exceptions, trends.
