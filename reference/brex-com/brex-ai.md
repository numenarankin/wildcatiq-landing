## Section: FAQ (9)
- What is intelligent finance? The next step beyond automation: AI agents rethink finance operations, act autonomously, keep human oversight.
- How is it different from automation? Automation speeds up steps; intelligent finance removes steps, learns patterns, adapts.
- How does AI enable it? ML for anomaly detection and forecasting, NLP for documents and conversation, predictive analytics for cash flow.
- Does it mean finance loses control? No: transparency, auditable decisions, automatic policy enforcement, humans on key decisions.
- How does Brex ensure compliance? Policies enforced at point of purchase, audit trails, 98% compliance rate claimed.
- Is it secure? Encryption, secure APIs, audit trails; financial data not used to train third-party AI models.
- What size company benefits? Startups to enterprises.
- What are AI agents? Systems that act autonomously; Brex agents trained on your policies and SOPs, accept feedback.
- What are the main uses? Expense management, compliance, auditing, exception handling, invoice processing, expense matching, journal entries, month-end reconciliation.
