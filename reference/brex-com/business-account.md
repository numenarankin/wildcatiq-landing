## Section: FAQ (15)
- How do I open a Brex business account? Three sub-accounts: Checking (Column N.A.; unlimited free same-day ACH, checks, domestic wires; up to 240 account numbers; FDIC up to $250,000), Treasury (Dreyfus Government Cash Management Fund, DGVXX, via Brex Treasury LLC, FINRA/SEC broker-dealer, SIPC member), Vault (Brex Treasury LLC; funds across 24 program banks for up to $6M FDIC coverage).
- How is Brex different from a traditional business bank account? Checking via Column N.A.; deposit checks, send/receive ACH, check, wire incl. international; Treasury and Vault add yield options.
- Where is my cash actually stored? Brex doesn't hold customer funds on its balance sheet except funds in transit. Checking at Column N.A.; swept nightly to Treasury (money market fund administered by BNY Mellon) or Vault program banks. Fund is AAA-rated, at least 99.5% cash or U.S. government-backed securities.
- Are my deposits FDIC-insured? Checking up to $250,000; Vault up to $6M in the customer's name; Treasury shares held in the customer's name; SIPC and FINRA can access records to return funds.
- What do I need to open an account? Business address, incorporation details, owner personal information incl. SSN or photo ID, backup withholding status.
- Can non-U.S. residents apply? Yes, with existing or planned U.S. operations and a principal place of business address (no registered agent, PO box, UPS box).
- How much does it cost? Free to open; no treasury transaction fees, minimums, overdraft, monthly, or hidden fees.
- Is Brex the best business checking account for startups? Checking, treasury, and Vault in one; no-minimum yield; bill pay.
- What factors should I consider when choosing a startup bank? Yield from the first dollar, 20x FDIC coverage, no treasury transaction fees, up to 40x higher limits.
- How does the treasury account help earn yield? Yield from account opening; auto-transfers; pairing with a Brex card gives up to 40x higher credit limits.
- What makes Brex a top option for growing companies? Easy onboarding, one place for checking/treasury/vault, 24/7 support, yield without thresholds, up to 20x FDIC coverage through 20+ program banks.
- How can I open an account in minutes? Same documents; quick if details are correct.
- Does Brex offer invoicing? Yes: create, send, track, reconcile.
- Does Brex offer a treasury account? Yes: U.S. government money market fund, same-hour liquidity, no minimum.
- How does it compare to traditional checking? Traditional checking doesn't put cash to work; Brex adds treasury and up to $6M FDIC via Vault.
