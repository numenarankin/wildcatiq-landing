## Section: Customize your spend limits and controls.
- Merchants and categories: block unwanted categories and restrict spend to specific vendors
- Max transaction amounts: set a maximum and auto-decline anything over it
- Virtual card only: block a limit from being used by a physical card or through reimbursements
- Limit increase requests: members can request higher limits
- Limit flexibility: add buffer room for overages or set a hard cap
- Hide total limit: members can't see the total limit or request increases

## Section: FAQ (3)
- What types of controls are available with spend limits? Individual (e.g. $5,000 per employee) or shared (e.g. $5,000 across employees); one-time or recurring; set for approved vendors.
- Can I block spending from specific merchants? Yes, e.g. a $100 per employee per month work-from-home stipend restricted to an approved vendor list.
- How do spend limits work with budgets? Plan budgets across the organization and build spend limits to track actuals: budgets by department with sub-budgets; limits with embedded policies for travel, stipends and perks, vendor payments; multiple limits and policies on one card; real-time multi-currency tracking; automated compliance.
