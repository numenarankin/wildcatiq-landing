- Presets: A set for every part of your day. Build presets with their own name, icon and apps. Swipe between work, sleep or dinner and start the right one in a second.

- Wind-down: Focus that starts on its own. Choose a time in the evening and the session starts without you. Your apps are still blocked when you wake up, until you scan.
- Websites: Block the web on your Mac too. Choose the websites from your iPhone, and the same session blocks them on your Mac too.
- Insights: See the time you get back. Total focus time, today, this week and this month. Your current and best streak, your longest session, and the hours you focus most.
- Leaderboard: Compete, if you want to. Off by default. Turn it on to see how your focus time compares, and race the people who did the same.
- Achievements: Streaks and milestones. Badges unlock as your focus time adds up.
- Coming soon: Focus that knows where you are. Set a place on the map and the session starts the moment you arrive. To stop it, scan your Norma there or leave the area.

## Section: Questions we get. (FAQ)
- Does Norma work on Android? No. Norma works on iPhone, with a companion app on Mac. If you're on Android, Brick supports both systems.
- What's the real difference with Brick? The principle is identical: an object you tap that cuts the apps you chose. The concrete differences are the Mac (Norma applies the block on the computer), automatic triggering by place or time, and the fact that Norma is iPhone-only where Brick also covers Android.
- Is there a subscription? No. The disc is bought once and the app is included, with no subscription and no in-app purchase.
- How is this different from Screen Time? Screen Time is switched off in seconds in Settings. Norma builds on Screen Time but makes unlocking depend on an object: if the disc is in another room, the apps stay blocked.
- Can I change my mind after buying? Yes. You have 14 days to return your disc under the European right of withdrawal.
