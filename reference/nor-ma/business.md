## Section: It runs on each person's phone.
- Norma works through Screen Time on each person's own phone, so there's nothing to deploy centrally and nothing to monitor. Everyone keeps their privacy.
- Deep-work sprints: Protect 90-minute focus blocks across the whole team, all starting at the same time.
- Phone-free meetings: Everyone scans before sitting down, so nobody spends the meeting half-scrolling.
- Healthier boundaries: Make logging off after hours the default, and reduce the risk of burnout.

## Section: How it rolls out.
- Order in bulk: One shipment, to a single address, or to each of your locations.
- Hand them out: Share the app link. Each person pairs their own phone in under a minute.
- Scan to focus: Then each person scans to block their apps and scans again to get them back.
