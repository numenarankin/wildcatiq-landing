## Section: Mac questions. (FAQ)
- Which Macs does the app support? The Norma Mac app runs on macOS 13 (Ventura) and later, on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
- Is the Mac app free? Yes. It's a free companion to your Norma disc. One account links your iPhone and your Mac. You only ever pay once, for the disc itself.
- Can Norma see or log my browsing? No. Blocking happens locally on your Mac. Norma never sees, logs or routes your browsing. It only knows which sites you chose to block.
- Will it slow down my Mac? No. The app sits in your menu bar and blocks at the network level, so there's no heavy background process and no noticeable performance hit.
- Which browsers does it cover? All of them. Because blocking happens at the network level, Safari, Chrome, Arc, Firefox and any other browser are covered automatically.
- Do I still need my iPhone to start a session? Yes. You start and end sessions by scanning your Norma disc with your iPhone. Your Mac mirrors whatever session is active.
