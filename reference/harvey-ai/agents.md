# Harvey - Agents product page reference

## Structure
- Single-feature page, about 9 sections: hero, one benefit statement, three feature groups (3 / 3 / 2 cards each, every card an H4 title + one sentence), a "Memory" feature callout, a 3-stat row, 5 customer quotes, a 5-item "How teams use agents" use-case tabs/carousel, security block, closing CTA.
- Depth per feature: one sentence each, always "what it does + why it matters for you". No sub-pages per feature; the page is the whole explanation.
- Copy to screenshot ratio: roughly one product screenshot per feature group plus one per use case (alt text describes the UI, e.g. "AI chat messages discussing a DRL with a checklist of tasks and Word documents for context"). Copy carries the page; screenshots illustrate.
- Tabs/accordions: the use-case block is a tab/carousel (titles repeat in the markup as a second set). No FAQ.
- Handoff to adjacent features: "Connectors" card points at Connector Library, "Memory" callout points to /memory, and the Platform mega-menu lists every sibling product with a one-line description. Same security block and closing CTA as the homepage.

## Section: Take on more at once.
- Statement: Take on more at once. Deploy a team of agents that split large tasks, work in parallel, and return review-ready outputs.

## Section: Get More Done with Agents
- Intro: Your team's hours go to clients, negotiations, and strategy while agents run work in the background.
- Parallel Execution: Hand off one task or ten. Harvey coordinates multiple agents to complete work in parallel and deliver review-ready outputs.
- Scheduled Agents: Keep work moving behind the scenes by automatically running reports, updates, and reviews on the schedule you choose.
- Multi-Format Support: Give agents documents, images, video, or audio, and get work product back as reports, presentations, interactive pages, or whatever the task requires.

## Section: Control the Quality of Work
- Intro: Shape the plan before it runs and review the work before it goes out. The judgment is always yours.
- Review and Approve Work: Preview the plan, adjust the scope, and approve work before Harvey begins.
- Nudges: Harvey proactively brings you in when your judgment is needed, then keeps work moving.
- Auditability: Every claim is backed by a citation and every step is logged, making it easy to verify accuracy before partner or client review.

## Section: Built to Your Standard
- Intro: Embed your IP into agents that work the way you do, drawing from your knowledge, playbooks, and preferences.
- Agent Builder: Build custom agents that capture your expertise, delivering consistent work without repeated instructions.
- Connectors: Give agents secure access to Microsoft, DMS systems, and the other tools your team already uses, so they can find information and complete work across systems.

- Contract drafting from precedent: Update precedent contract language for a new deal based on term sheet requirements while maintaining firm standards.
- Case strategy development: Synthesize case materials into a strategy memo, pressure-test your arguments, and refine the narrative before advising your client on next steps.
