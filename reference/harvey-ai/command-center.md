# Harvey - Command Center product page reference

## Structure
- About 6 sections: hero, a three-verb value statement ("See, understand, act"), three feature groups mapped to those verbs (4 / 3 / 2 cards), 7 customer quotes, 4-item use-case tabs, closing CTA. No stats, no security block.
- Depth: one sentence per card. The organizing device is the verb triad; each group heading restates one verb.
- Copy to screenshot: a screenshot per feature block; copy leads.
- Tabs/accordions: use-case block tabbed; the mega-menu is a dropdown. No FAQ.
- Social proof is the heaviest of any Harvey product page (7 named quotes) because the buyer is an Innovation / Legal Ops lead, not a practitioner.
- Handoff: "Build Your Own ROI Calculator" use case points at the Command Center Agent; "Recommended features" hands to the rest of the platform.

## Section: See, understand, act.
- Statement: See, understand, act. Get peer benchmarks, instant answers, and recommended next steps in one unified workspace.

## Section: See where you stand
- Intro: Measure adoption across your organization and benchmark against peers to understand what's working and where to improve.
- Usage insights: Track adoption by feature and user to understand what's driving engagement and identify your power users.
- Granular breakdown: Compare adoption across practice group, role, region, and more to identify where to focus training and support.
- User Profile Sync via SCIM: Sync employee profile attributes into Harvey, keeping this data up to date automatically and using your firm's own terminology in analytics and beyond.
- Peer benchmarking: See how your adoption compares to similar organizations to understand where you're ahead - and where you can improve.

## Section: Understand why
- Intro: Ask Harvey about your data to uncover trends, explain results, and identify where to focus next.
- Ask the assistant: Ask questions about anything from firm-wide adoption to individual users and get answers in minutes.
- Query history: Revisit, rerun, and build on previous questions to track changes over time.
- Create deliverables: Turn answers into reports, presentations, dashboards, or interactive pages that are ready to share.

## Section: Act on what's next
- Intro: Get recommended capabilities and manage access from one place.
- Recommended features: Explore the latest Harvey capabilities and decide what to enable next.
- Manage access: Grant or adjust access for users and roles directly from Command Center as your rollout evolves.
