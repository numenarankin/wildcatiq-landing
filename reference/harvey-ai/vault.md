# Harvey - Vault product page reference

## Structure
- Single-feature page, about 7 sections: hero, one-line value statement, three feature groups of exactly 3 cards each (H3 group heading + intro sentence, then H4 card + one sentence), 3-stat row, one testimonial, 6-item use-case tabs, closing CTA. No security block on this page.
- Depth: every capability gets one sentence; concrete limits and names are used as copy (100,000 documents; iManage, SharePoint, Google Drive; Assistant, Workflows, Mobile, Word and Outlook Add-Ins).
- Copy to screenshot: one hero image plus one screenshot per feature card and per use case; copy leads.
- Tabs/accordions: use-case block is tabbed (titles repeat in markup). No FAQ.
- Handoff: the "Cross-platform connection" card names the sibling surfaces vaults appear in; "Knowledge bases" overlaps with the Knowledge page. Mega-menu and footer link every sibling.

- Intro: Compare key data points from thousands of documents at once in a structured, tabular format.

- Synthesize extracted data points: Ask questions over any review table to summarize, compare, or isolate key insights.

## Section: Accelerate reviews in shared vaults
- Intro: Unify content across files, emails, queries, and Document Management System integrations in secure vaults.
- Vaults: Securely store and organize up to 100,000 documents including files, email correspondence, and queries in vaults that are accessible across Harvey's platform.
- DMS integrations: Sync materials from document management systems like iManage, SharePoint, and Google Drive to centralize content.
- Sharing: Collaborate in vaults and review tables with colleagues and external partners - with full control over who can access, view, and edit.

## Section: Drive consistency across workstreams
- Intro: Leverage your organization's institutional knowledge to draft, review, and deliver consistent legal work at scale.
- Knowledge bases: Drive consistency across your organization with curated repositories of internal knowledge like precedents, templates, and playbooks.
- Permissions & governance controls: Control who can access, edit, and view vaults and knowledge bases.
- Cross-platform connection: Leverage vaults across Harvey's platform, including Assistant, Workflows, Mobile, and Harvey's Word and Outlook Add-Ins.
