## Section: One front door for every ask
- Statement: One front door for every ask with intake, routing, and review in one place. Resolve work faster and focus judgment where it matters.

## Section: Meet the Business Where It Works
- Intro: Business teams ask Legal in the tools they already use, and get answers back in the same place.
- Connected channels: Capture requests from Email, Jira, Slack, ServiceNow, iManage, Google Drive, SharePoint and more, with full context and attachments, and send updates back to the source.
- Guest experience: Business users can open their request, add details, ask questions, check status, and see the result, without a Harvey seat.
- Channel updates: Send every update, follow-up question, and answer to the requester in their original channel.

## Section: Give Your Team a Head Start
- Intro: Harvey takes the first pass on every request, so lawyers start with the context and a draft, not a blank page.
- Intelligent routing: Match each request to an approved agent, a solution like Contract Intelligence, or a lawyer, based on rules your team defines.
- Agent execution: Harvey gathers missing context, runs the approved agent, and brings in the right lawyer when review is needed.
- Contract Intelligence handoff: Incoming contracts go straight into Contract Intelligence for setup and first-pass review, then stay tracked through negotiation, signature, and renewal.

## Section: Stay in Control of Every Request
- Intro: Set the rules for routing, access, and review, with a clear owner and status on every request.
- Full audit trail: See a clear record of every request, including Harvey's work, the team's review, and the final response sent back to the business.
- Team queues: Assign and rebalance incoming work from a shared triage queue, and give each lawyer a clear view of their requests in My Tasks.
- Set the rules once: Define the requests you support, the details each one needs, and when Harvey escalates. Assign default owners and reviewers by request type.

## Section: Built for Legal Work Across the Business (tabs, each with a screenshot)
- Review sales contracts: Route inbound MSAs and order forms from Sales to Contract Intelligence for redlines, so deals keep moving while a lawyer handles the exceptions.
- Clear vendor agreements: Run Procurement's supplier contracts and DPAs against your approved positions, and escalate only material deviations to a reviewer.
- Answer employment questions: Resolve routine HR policy questions with approved agents, and send cross-border hiring or contractor questions to employment counsel with full context.
- Approve marketing claims: Send campaign copy and claims through Legal's approved review process, and escalate anything new or high-risk to the right lawyer
