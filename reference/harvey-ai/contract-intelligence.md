- Statement: Stay ahead of every contract. Connect contract intake, review, and insights in one system to stay in control as review volume grows.

## Section: Accelerate contract intake and reviews
- Intro: Triage your inbound contracts from the systems your business already uses, and agents finish the first pass before a reviewer opens the file.
- Contract Intake: Capture inbound contracts automatically from synced vaults, forwarding to a workspace email, and systems like Jira and ServiceNow, so nothing waits in an inbox.
- Contract Review Agents: Harvey detects the contract type, gathers deal context, and runs a review grounded in your standards in minutes.
- Review Queue & Assignment: Keep ownership clear by assigning contracts based on your rules, tracking everything in flight, and giving every reviewer a clean queue to start the day.

## Section: Negotiate from your strongest positions
- Intro: Convert successful terms into playbook and precedent bank updates that strengthen the next deal.
- Playbook & Agent Updates: Get recommendations on when to update a playbook or review agent, backed by precedent data showing where your executed deals have moved past your written positions.
- Playbooks: Review against your standards, not a generic template, with playbooks built from your precedents and refined until they review the way your best lawyer does.
- Precedent Benchmarking: Visualize how your current negotiation compares to your precedent with clause-level data visualizations.

## Section: Stay ahead of risks and obligations
- Intro: Surface risk exposure, obligations, and turnaround time across every contract the business signs.
- Contract Insights: See how your liability caps, IP terms, and governing law positions trend across the portfolio, and where they're drifting from market.
- Obligation & Key Dates: Surface renewals, notice periods, and commitments that need attention before they become problems.
- Leadership Reporting: Walk into board conversations with a clear view of risk exposure and negotiation patterns across the contract portfolio.

## Section: How Teams Use Contract Intelligence (tabs, each with a screenshot)
- Review inbound contracts: Auto-triage inbound contracts like MSAs from your business systems, run your playbook, and return redlines the same hour they arrive.
- Benchmark deal terms: Compare a live negotiation against the terms you've actually accepted in executed deals, not what the template says.
- Manage renewals and commitments: Identify upcoming dates, obligations, and terms that should be revisited before the business renews.
- Streamline contract queues: See every review in one place, assign work, and give each reviewer a clear view of what needs attention.
- Report on contracting: Give legal leadership a portfolio-level view of risk, negotiation patterns, review volume, and turnaround time.
