## Section: Value Props
- Group: Bringing parties together to execute on multi-faceted projects. Internal teams, outside counsel, and clients working on a unified set of context, in a single environment, from first draft to final work product.
  - Spaces: A governed environment where every collaborator - firm, client, or internal team - works from the same context, with the same tools, on the same matter.
  - Shared Vaults and Artifacts: Consolidate all matter-relevant documents into a queryable knowledge base and co-build together.
  - Space Analytics: Real-time visibility into who's active, what they're running, and how your shared work is being used.
- Group: Share your expertise. Publish AI workflow agents directly into a Space. A collaborator runs them on demand without onboarding and back-and-forth.
  - Shared Workflows: Collaborators run structured analysis at scale such as NDA reviews, regulatory assessments, and diligence queries directly from a unified Space.
  - Playbooks: Embed your methodology into reusable Playbooks that deliver consistent, high-quality outputs - offering a new means for delivering value while preserving proprietary know-how and extending reach.
  - Guest accounts: Enable external selected collaborators to access Spaces with scoped, admin-controlled permissions without requiring full Harvey platform access.
- Group: Deep collaboration with built-in governance. The work that happens in Spaces is often privileged, sensitive, and high-stakes. Harvey's governance framework makes deep collaboration possible.
  - Granular access controls: Permissions are set at the resource level, not just the Space level. Nothing reaches a collaborator without explicit admin approval from both sides.
  - Ethical wall enforcement: Intapp integration enforces ethical walls automatically across matters - so you can collaborate across organizational lines without compromising conflict controls.
  - Full audit trails: Every access grant, resource share, query, and approval action is logged in real time. Admins have complete visibility across all Spaces, users, and externally shared resources.

## Section: Built for the way legal teams work (tabs, each with a screenshot)
- Matter Workspace: Consolidate all documents, workflows, and work product for a matter into a single Space - keeping cross-functional stakeholders aligned and eliminating version confusion.
- Institutional Knowledge Base: Build shared Vault of precedents, playbooks, and prior work product that every lawyer on the team can query in natural language - institutional knowledge that doesn't walk out the door.
- M&A Due Diligence: Firm and in-house team upload VDR content into a shared Vault, query for red flags together, and build a live review table - without emailing documents back and forth.
- NDA Review at Scale: Deploy a workflow so collaborators can review inbound NDAs or markups on demand, running structured analysis at scale without opening a new matter for each one.
- LPA and Side Letter Library: A funds practice builds a structured library of key economic terms, restrictions, and obligations. Clients query it directly - getting instant answers on fund mechanics without a call.
- Regulatory Filing Workspace: Firm and client jointly draft regulatory submissions, map new requirements against existing policies, and route revisions through approval chains - all in one governed environment.
