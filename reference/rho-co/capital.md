## Section: How Rho's working capital line of credit works (4 steps)
- Apply from your Rho account: Give us the standard business details on file, your EIN, formation documents, and ownership information, along with a look at recent cash flow.
- Underwriting reviews your business: We look at the shape of the business today rather than checking it against a fixed, one-size checklist. Depending on underwriting, approval may also require a personal guaranty from a business owner.
- Get approved: Once approved, your line is ready. There's no separate login and no new account to manage; it lives alongside your existing Rho account.
- Draw, repay, and redraw: Funds land in your Rho account in as little as 24-48 hours after approval. Repay each draw on your own schedule, up to 180 days, then draw again as you need to.

## Section: In-depth guides
- What a working capital line of credit is: A revolving facility: draw, repay, and redraw as cash needs shift. Rho gives qualifying startups up to $5M, funded in 24 to 48 hours after approval, with up to 180 days to repay each draw. Bullets: draw at any time; repaying restores available credit; up to $5M, larger case by case; terms up to 180 days per draw. (visual: $5,000,000 available to draw; repaying restores it)
- Working capital line of credit vs. venture debt: A working capital line is short-term and operational (cover a slow month, bridge a late customer payment, drawn and repaid again). Venture debt is a larger, longer facility built to extend runway alongside a funding round, typically from a specialized lender. Bullets: venture debt typically a term loan with equity warrants; Rho Capital revolving with per-draw terms up to 180 days; venture debt sized off your last equity round, Rho Capital sized by underwriting; a revolving line fits repeatable short-cycle credit. (visual: average balance $0.5M vs $2.7M over 48 months)
- Who qualifies: startups and LLCs: Evaluated through underwriting on the shape of the business today, not years in operation or a revenue floor. LLCs, corporations, and newer companies without years of financial history can all apply. You'll need an EIN, formation documents, and ownership details. Startups with only an EIN and no trading history are a common case. Link: Rho Incorporation. (visual: term loan does not refill, line of credit refills)
- How to apply for a working capital line of credit: Apply from your Rho account with EIN, formation documents, ownership, and recent cash flow; underwriting reviews; once approved, draw immediately; draw, repay, redraw for up to 180 days per draw without reapplying. (visual: MCA lender takes money every day; with Rho Capital you pick the day, 180 to repay)
