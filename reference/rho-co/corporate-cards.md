## Section: SPENDING CONTROLS / Which corporate card controls does Rho offer?
- Rho Corporate Cards let admins set a limit type, a merchant list of up to 20, merchant categories, company-wide restrictions, International Spend, usage dates and Lock and Unlock. A purchase outside a card's merchant list, merchant categories, Recurring Limit or usage dates, a non-US purchase on a card with International Spend off, and any attempt on a locked card are all declined. A long list matters less than whether a control actually stops the charge.

## Section: RHO CARD CONTROLS table (toggle: What it does / Where an admin sets it)
- Recurring Limit: Declines any transaction that would push the card past its limit. Monthly limits reset on the 1st. | Card Limit Type field at creation; edit in Configure Card > Card Limit.
- Fixed Limit: Virtual card spends up to one dollar amount in total; locks automatically once settled. | Choose Fixed in Card Limit Type.
- Single-Use: One-time usage limit on a virtual card; cannot be changed after creation. | Choose Single-Use in Card Limit Type.
- Merchant list: Restricts a card to up to 20 distinct merchants. | Cards > All Cards > the card > Configure Card > Spend Controls.
- Merchant categories: Restricts to Mastercard-defined categories (Advertising Services, Airlines, Grocery Stores). | ... > Spend Controls on > Categories tab.
- Company-wide restrictions: Blocks merchants and categories on every Rho Card (unlimited categories, up to 20 merchants). | Settings > Security > Card Restrictions > Change.
- International Spend: On by default; off declines non-US transactions. | Card Settings > Advanced Controls > International Spend.
- Usage dates: Card works only between set dates; cards expire 3 years after issue by default. | Configure Card > Card Usage Dates.
- Lock and Unlock: Inactivates a card until unlocked. | three-dot menu > Lock Card.
- Vendor Cards: Dedicated cards for vendor payments with merchant restrictions and usage dates at setup. | Cards > Create Card > Vendor Card, singly or bulk by CSV; single-use card tied to a bill via Bill Pay payment method; web only.
- Caption: Controls as of October 2026; roles that can set categories; link to "Why Was My Rho Card Declined?"

## Section: CONTROLS CHECKLIST / What controls should companies set on corporate card usage?
- Intro: A corporate card policy should set at least these controls. Start narrow and loosen only when a team asks.
- 1. Put a limit on every card. 2. Give recurring vendors their own card (Vendor Card). 3. Use Fixed or Single-Use for one-off purchases. 4. Restrict by merchant or category where the use is predictable. 5. Block what no one should buy (company-wide restrictions). 6. Switch off International Spend on cards that never leave the US. 7. Set usage dates for contractors, event budgets and anything with an end date. 8. Lock a card first, then investigate.

## Section: IN-DEPTH GUIDE (4 mini-essays)
- What is a corporate credit card and how does it work? Issued to a company rather than an individual; business liable; Rho underwrites the business, settles on Daily or Monthly Terms.
- Types of corporate cards: charge card (paid in full each cycle), purchasing/P-card (vendor and procurement spend); Rho settles on Daily or Monthly Terms.
- Corporate card vs. business credit card: "business credit card" often means a card issued to the owner personally with a personal guarantee; "corporate card" implies company-level underwriting.
- How to choose a corporate card program: start with eligibility (minimum balance, revenue, personal guarantee), then compare real Cashback mechanics (cash or points, what spend counts).
