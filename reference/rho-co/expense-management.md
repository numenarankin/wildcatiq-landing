## Section: Built so you don't have to become a finance operator
- Your team moves. You don't slow it down: Set approval rules by amount or team and let the right person review the right spend. Stay in the loop on what matters, skip the rest. (visual: approval chain requiring Josh H., then either Oliver R. or Rachel F.)
- The rules apply before the money leaves: Off-policy spend gets flagged the moment the card is used, not when you're closing the books. Set it once and Rho handles it every time. (visual: travel policy rule for airline spend requiring a receipt and a reason memo)
- Stop being the receipt cop: Your team uploads and codes receipts the second they spend. Books close without a single Slack asking for documentation. (visual: receipt uploaded, approvals from Jamie S., then Mark R. or Susan W.)

## Section: What is expense management?
- Rho expense management is the set of tools that control card spend, capture receipts, route approvals, and code transactions for your accounting software, in one platform with no per-user or platform fees. It sits between your spend and your books, so spend gets controlled before it happens instead of reported after the fact. Coded transactions sync to your accounting software the moment they close, so the books stay current without a separate export step.
- Bullets: Corporate cards, spend controls, and automated reconciliation in one platform; No per-user or platform fees; Coded transactions sync to your accounting software the moment they close
- CTA: Apply Now

## Section: How to set up expense management with Rho
- 01 Issue cards instantly: Create a virtual or physical card for any employee or vendor, with limits set before the first swipe.
- 02 Reconcile in your books: Coded, receipted transactions sync to QuickBooks Online, NetSuite, Sage Intacct, or Puzzle.

## Section: How to choose an expense management platform
- Start with the fee schedule. A platform that charges per user gets more expensive exactly as the team that needs expense management most starts to grow. Check whether the accounting integrations you actually use are native or a lighter bank-feed connection, since that difference determines how much manual work survives the "automation." Confirm any cashback claim states a real annual cap and doesn't quietly let the top tier read as the standard rate.
- Bullets: $0 per user and per platform, on every plan; Native integrations: QuickBooks Online, NetSuite, Sage Intacct, and Puzzle; Cashback up to 2% on Daily Terms with Rho Platinum (terms apply), 1.25% standard on Daily Terms, on up to $1,000,000 in eligible annual card spend
