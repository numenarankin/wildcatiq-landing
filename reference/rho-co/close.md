## Section: How it works / Close gets faster every month
- Intro: Rho already knows your books. Click to generate a first pass, review what's right, and sync. No manual data entry. No starting from scratch every month.
- Rho learns your books: Your chart of accounts, your vendors, your coding history. Not a generic model. Yours. (visual: Account, Department, Vendor labels)
- Rho takes the first pass: Open the accounting view and click to generate. A suggestion for every transaction, ready to review. (visual: expense table with AWS, Delta Airlines, Uber, Figma and categories)

## Section: Month-end close in minutes, not hours.
- Rho Close is month-end close software that drafts a suggested account category for every transaction, using your chart of accounts, your vendor history, and your past coding decisions. It is built for founders who do their own books and for accountants who manage one startup or ten. You generate suggestions on demand, review them, and override anything. It is not a general ledger, and it does not replace your accountant.
