## Section: How the accounts payable process works with Rho Bill Pay (4 steps)
- Forward the invoice: Send a vendor invoice to your Rho Bill Pay inbox instead of entering it by hand.
- Review the draft bill: Rho Bill Pay drafts the bill for review in a dedicated Rho inbox, no separate logins required.
- Route for approval: Send the bill for approval within Rho Bill Pay.
- Pay and sync: Once approved, pay by ACH, domestic wire, or check. The payment syncs to your accounting software.

## Section: In-depth guides
- What is accounts payable automation? Software that handles invoice intake, approval, and vendor payment without manual data entry at every step. Rho Bill Pay is included with banking rather than sold as separate software; does not include procurement. Bullets: captures invoices and extracts vendor, amount, due date; routes for approval; pays by ACH, domestic wire, or check and syncs; no separate AP subscription.
- How AP automation works, step by step: four parts (invoice intake to a dedicated inbox; automated capture pulling vendor, amount, due date and flagging duplicates; approval routing by your rules; payment and sync). Sub-points: invoice capture (OCR, duplicates flagged before payment); approval workflows (approvers can review from the Rho mobile app); vendor payments (ACH, domestic wire, or check from Rho Checking; Rho prints and mails paper checks on your behalf).
- Manual AP vs. automated AP: For a handful of bills a month, manual is fine; once volume or approver headcount grows, late and duplicate payments start. Rows: invoice entry (typed vs captured), approvals (email/printed sheet vs routed), duplicate payments (caught after vs flagged before), bookkeeping (reconciled by hand vs synced as paid).
- How to choose accounts payable automation software: start with total cost (subscription plus per-payment fees); check payment methods; confirm approval controls fit your team; decide whether AP is bundled with the bank account or separate software.
