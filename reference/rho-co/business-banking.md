## Section: HOW IT WORKS / How to open a business bank account online
- Check eligibility: Rho serves US-incorporated businesses with a US operating address, or one US-based owner with a valid SSN. Sole proprietorships are not served.
- Have your EIN ready: Keep your EIN on hand before you apply.
- Apply online: Complete the Rho application online.
- Fund and start banking: Send ACH and wires and connect your accounting software.
- Callout "No EIN yet?": Have your EIN ready before you apply. If you do not have one yet, Rho's guide explains what to do first. Link: Read the guide (blog: open-business-bank-account-before-ein).

## Section: IN-DEPTH GUIDE / What to know before you open
- Intro: A business account keeps company money apart from personal money. Here is who Rho is built for, and how to compare your options. Sub-guides: Who Rho business banking is built for; Business bank account for an LLC; Online platform or branch bank?; How to choose a small business bank account.
- Who Rho business banking is built for (table Built for / Why): Finance teams that sync to accounting software (native NetSuite, QuickBooks Online, Sage Intacct, Puzzle) | Startups that want one platform ($0 monthly, per-user, minimum balance fees; expense management with no user fees) | Teams that organize cash by purpose (checking sub-accounts for payroll, taxes, and more) | LLCs and corporations formed in the US (US operating address or one US-based owner with SSN)
- Business bank account for an LLC: An LLC needs its own business account to keep company money separate; Rho serves US-incorporated LLCs and corporations, not sole proprietorships; no monthly, per-user, or minimum balance fees so costs stay flat; native accounting connections. Link: Business bank accounts for LLC owners.
- Online platform or branch bank?: Choose a branch bank if your business handles significant physical cash or wants in-person service. Choose an online platform if your business runs digitally and wants payments, cards, and accounting connections in one place. Rho is an online banking platform, not a bank. Link: Online business bank accounts compared.
- How to choose a small business bank account: Check five things. Fees (Rho $0 monthly, per-user, minimum balance); Deposit insurance (which bank holds deposits, FDIC limit per entity); Software (which tools connect natively); Support (hours and channels; Rho 24/7 live human); Eligibility (entity type; Rho serves US-incorporated businesses). Link: Business checking for startups.
