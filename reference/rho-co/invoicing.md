## Section: In-depth guides (01 to 04)
- 01 What is invoicing software? Replaces manually drafted bills with a system that creates, sends, and tracks invoices in one place. Tools differ in whether an invoice can repeat on its own, whether payment gets matched back automatically, and where the money lands. Bullets: creates and sends without manual work; matches payments to open invoices; recurring invoices on your schedule; no separate subscription.
- 02 How invoice automation works: create, send, collect, reconcile. Electronic invoicing (branded invoices with a portal on every invoice); Payment collection ($0 Rho fees on domestic ACH, wire, check; card 2.9% + $0.30); Recurring invoices; Cash application (automatic payment matching).
- 03 Accounts receivable automation, explained: covers the invoice-to-cash cycle. Rho handles create, send, and reconcile inside the account a business already banks with. Standalone tools: payments land in a connected external bank account. Invoicing built into banking (Rho): payments land in Rho checking, automatic matching. Links: See how Rho compares with other B2B invoicing software; with other small-business invoicing software.
- 04 How to choose invoicing software for your business: start with the fee schedule (some charge a percentage of every paid invoice); check how customers can pay; look at where the money lands. Bullets: fee schedule compounds on every dollar; feature list (recurring, payment methods, auto reconciliation); money lands in the same account invoices go out from; Rho is built for startups and small businesses wanting invoicing and banking in one platform.
