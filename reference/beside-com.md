## Section: Your questions, covered (FAQ)
- CTA: Get started today / Get started
- What exactly is Beside? An AI operating system for small local businesses. It brings everything you need to win and keep customers into one place that runs on its own: a business phone line, AI receptionist, lead follow-up, CRM, website, reputation, ads, and scheduling. Most tools give you a dashboard and leave the work to you; Beside actually does the work.
- Do I have to replace the tools I already use? No. Beside works with the tools you already rely on, like your CRM, your email, your phone number, and your calendar, so you keep what works and let Beside run the rest.
- Will it do things without my permission? You decide how much it runs alone. Routine low-risk work runs on its own from day one; anything with your name on it arrives as a draft you approve, edit, or reject. Approve the same move again and again and it offers to take it over. Every action has an undo.
- Do I have to change my phone number? No. You can port your existing number to Beside or forward calls from your current carrier. Your callers keep dialing the same number and nothing changes on their end.
- How is the waitlist order decided? By tier first, then join date. Connect your Google Business Profile to skip everyone still in Tier 1; refer a business that connects theirs to skip everyone in Tier 2. We email you every time you move.
- Why do you want my Google Business Profile? So your free Business Audit is ready the day you get access: Beside studies your profile, reviews, and market, and writes your growth plan before launch. Read-only, we never post without your approval, and you can disconnect anytime.
- What is the Beside Ad Credit? One business from the first 1,000 waitlist signups receives the Beside Ad Credit: $1,000 in Beside credits, real money against your bill, to put toward the growth plan its audit lays out. Entry is automatic when you join, the winner is selected at random, and nothing you do or buy changes your odds. Official rules are linked below the signup form.
- What does Beside cost? Part-time is $99/month with 5,000 credits. Full-time is $249.99/month with 12,500 credits. Both plans use the same credit rates. Prices shown are USD. Start with a free business audit. Your available offer and terms are shown before subscribing. Standalone Beside Phone plans are listed at beside.com/phone/pricing. Exploring Beside does not change an existing Phone subscription.
- How is Beside different from Beside Phone? Beside is an agent for your business, with its own workspace and credit-based plans. Beside Phone is the standalone app for your business number, AI Receptionist, calls, and messages. Use the same Beside account to sign in; your access determines which businesses and products you can open. Your existing Phone subscription continues. Opening Beside does not move your number or change your plan.
