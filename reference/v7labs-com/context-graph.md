- Eyebrow: One context layer

## Section: Built for
- Partners & Analysts: Everyone works from the same context. No more chasing files or re-explaining what the fund already knows.
- Deal teams: Run diligence and track deal progression with full historical context across every transaction your firm has touched.
- Portfolio monitoring workflows: Extract and structure portfolio reporting as it arrives. Flag variances and track KPIs without normalising data by hand.
- Deal sourcing agents: Screen inbound opportunities against your investment criteria at scale, before a human needs to spend time on a pass.
- LP reporting workflows: Generate consistent LP reports from live portfolio data. Less time assembling packs, more time on the relationships that matter.
