## Section: Bring your KPIs. Stop telling agents what to say. Tell Scout what to improve.

- Images: Mountain valley at golden hour; Scout KPI console tracking a Resolution Rate metric over time

## Section: How Scout works. Scout is your own forward-deployed engineer on autopilot.
- Intro: The moment a KPI goes live, Scout learns from your real conversations. When a human has to intervene, Scout learns from it and ships fixes that move your metrics.
- 01 Define the KPI: In plain English. Set a target and how often to recompute.
- 02 Scout learns: Reads conversations and what reps did to resolve the hard ones.
- 03 Files the fix: Each idea becomes a typed item: policy, tooling, or knowledge.
- 04 Ships or routes: Tests safe changes on a slice of traffic. Routes risky ones to a human.
- 05 The KPI climbs: You approve the rollout. Scout self-heals broken connections.
- Mockup: KPIs > CSAT positive (Auto-improve). New KPI: Name "CSAT positive"; In plain English "Share of rated conversations where the customer gave 4 or 5 stars"; Target 80%, Higher is better; Recompute Daily; Create KPI.

## Section: Scout in Slack. Ask Scout in the channel. Approve the fix before it ships.
- Intro: One thread, from the first question to the shipped fix.
- 01 Ask in the channel: Mention Scout in any thread. It answers with your live numbers, measured against the target you set.
- 02 Get the why: Scout reads the conversations behind the number and clusters them. It names which group is the lever, and how far to trust the split.
- 03 Approve before anything ships: Scout names the step producing the problem and proposes the change. Nothing goes live until you say so.
- Mockup Slack thread (#support-ops): "CSAT dropped again yesterday and containment looks fine. Not sure what to make of it." / "@Scout pull contained and CSAT for yesterday. Can you also explain the theme around the ratings of 1?" / Scout: "Here is yesterday, closed out. Containment 82.6%, 5,120 chats, 890 escalated. CSAT positive 63.1%, 540 rated, 341 positive. Containment held in the 80s. CSAT is the one to look at: your target is 80%, so yesterday came in well under it. There are 118 one star ratings and 112 of them have summaries, so I can tell you why rather than guess. Want me to go through them?"

## Section: FAQs. Frequently asked questions
- Side link: Still have questions? Talk to our team
- How fast can we go live? Your first agent runs in about ten minutes. Most teams are in production in under two weeks. No multi-quarter rollout - it starts improving your KPI the same day.
- What happens when an agent cannot handle something? It hands off to your team with the full context of the conversation. You set the escalation rules in plain language, so it knows exactly when to step back.
- How does Giga's Scout work with our existing systems? It acts in your real tools and data, in real time. Agents read and write to your systems to resolve an issue end to end, not just answer questions.
- Is our data secure? SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and ISO 42001. Independently audited controls for security, privacy, and responsible AI.
- Does it handle other languages? 99 of them, with native-level fluency. One agent, every language your customers speak.
- How do you keep it accurate? Every agent is evaluated on your real conversations before launch, then monitored live. The improvement loop learns from the calls your team had to step into and fixes the agent so they happen less.
- Can we control its tone and policies? Yes. Set personality, policies, and guardrails in plain English, and change them anytime.
- How is it priced? Outcome-based and enterprise-scoped. We price to the number you want to move, and scope it with you at the demo.
