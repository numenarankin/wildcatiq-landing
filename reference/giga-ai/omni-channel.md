## Section: Voice. Phone support that sounds like your best agent, every call.
- Voice is the hardest channel to automate. Giga perfected it first - that engineering rigor carries through everywhere. Every call feels personal, informed, and resolved.
- Sub-second responses and real-time sentiment adaptation mean customers never wait, never repeat themselves, and never want to hang up.
- Mockup transcript: CUSTOMER "Hi, I need a refund." AGENT "Hey! Sure, let me just check your eligibility" CUSTOMER "Thanks a lot!"

## Section: Chat. Conversations that feel personal, not automated.
- Customers don't want to navigate a decision tree. They want to be understood, guided, and resolved - instantly.
- Resolution, not deflection. Process refunds, update accounts, apply discounts, manage subscriptions. No escalation queue, no "let me transfer you." The conversation IS the resolution.
- Mockup: capabilities chat ending in a browser permission request: "To continue, Giga needs temporary access to your browser. This is used only for the demonstration and can be stopped at any time. Would you like to grant access?"
- Callout: Every conversation is a revenue opportunity. The agent doesn't just close tickets - it spots upsells, catches churn signals, and recommends upgrades. Support stops being a cost center. It starts generating revenue.

## Section: Messaging. Be where your customers already are.
- SMS, WhatsApp, and more - the same intelligence that resolves complex voice calls, now in the channels customers reach for first.
- SMS, WhatsApp, and beyond. No separate bot, no separate logic. One agent with full resolution capability across every messaging platform your customers use.
- Faster answers in the channel customers prefer. Full resolution over text with complete conversation history - fewer calls, faster outcomes, lower cost to serve.
